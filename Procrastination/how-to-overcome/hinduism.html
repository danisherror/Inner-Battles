Procrastination

Putting off what matters until later, though you know the delay will cost you.

How to overcome it

The Gita's answer is steady, unattached action, not waiting for the mood.

  • Act. Verse 3.8: "action is superior to inaction."
  • Do the work as duty, without clinging to results. Verse 3.19 begins: "Therefore, giving up attachment, perform actions as a matter of duty". This removes the fear of the outcome that feeds delay.
  • Aim for the steady doer. Verse 18.26 describes the performer in the mode of goodness as one "free from egotism and attachment, endowed with enthusiasm and determination, and equipoised in success and failure."
  • Lift yourself. Verse 6.5: "Elevate yourself through the power of your mind, and not degrade yourself."
  • Keep the body regular. Verse 6.17 praises those "temperate in eating and recreation, balanced in work, and regulated in sleep".
  • Arise. Krishna's first word to Arjuna in 2.3 is: "Give up such petty weakness of heart and arise."
