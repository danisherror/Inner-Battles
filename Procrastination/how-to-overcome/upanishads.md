The Upanishadic call is to wake up now and to strive with strength.

- **Rise and awake.** Katha 1.3.14: "Rise, awake! having obtained your boons, understand them! The sharp edge of a razor is difficult to pass over; thus the wise say the path (to the Self) is hard." The call to rise is made together with the warning that the way takes effort.
- **Choose the good.** Katha 1.2.1-2: distinguish the good from the pleasant and cling to the good.
- **Take the reins.** Katha 1.3.6: "he who has understanding and whose mind is always firmly held, his senses are under control, like good horses of a charioteer."
- **Bring strength and earnestness.** Mundaka 3.2.4 says the Self is gained by one who strives "by strength, earnestness, and right meditation".
