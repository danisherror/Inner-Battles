Procrastination

How to overcome it

The Christian answer joins urgency with grace.

  • Make the most of the time. Ephesians 5:15-16: "Be careful, then, how you live, not as unwise people but as wise, making the most of the time."
  • Now. 2 Corinthians 6:2: "Look, now is the acceptable time; look, now is the day of salvation!"
  • Encourage one another daily. Hebrews 3:13: "But exhort one another every day, as long as it is called 'today'". Accountability is built into the text.
  • Work with your might. Ecclesiastes 9:10: "Whatever your hand finds to do, do with your might".
  • Do not tire of doing good. Galatians 6:9: "So let us not grow weary in doing what is right, for we will reap at harvest time, if we do not give up."
  • Receive help and grace. After describing his divided will, Paul asks, "Who will rescue me from this body of death?" and answers, "Thanks be to God through Jesus Christ our Lord!" (Romans 7:24-25). Romans 8:1 adds: "there is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus." Failure does not end the path.
