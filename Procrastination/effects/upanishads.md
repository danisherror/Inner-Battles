The Upanishads describe the result of heedlessness as going round in circles, without arriving.

- **Staggering in darkness.** Katha 1.2.5: "Fools dwelling in darkness, wise in their own conceit, and puffed up with vain knowledge, go round and round, staggering to and fro, like blind men led by the blind."
- **Falling under death's sway again and again.** After the careless child thinks "there is no other" world, "thus he falls again and again under my sway" (Katha 1.2.6).
- **The goal slips away.** Mundaka 3.2.4: the Self is not gained "by one who is destitute of strength, or without earnestness, or without right meditation."
- **The restless, uncontrolled mind never arrives.** Katha 1.3.7: one "who has no understanding, who is unmindful and always impure, never reaches that place, but enters into the round of births."
