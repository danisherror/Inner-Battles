Procrastination

Putting off what matters until later, though you know the delay will cost you.

Effects on life

Scripture describes delay as costing a person their harvest, and sometimes their chance.

  • Poverty and loss. Proverbs 6:10-11: "A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest, and poverty will come upon you like a robber, and want, like an armed warrior."
  • A missed harvest. Proverbs 20:4: "The lazy person does not plow in season; harvest comes, and there is nothing to be found."
  • Wanting without getting. Proverbs 13:4: "The appetite of the lazy craves and gets nothing, while the appetite of the diligent is richly supplied."
  • An uncertain tomorrow. Proverbs 27:1: "Do not boast about tomorrow, for you do not know what a day may bring." James 4:14 adds: "you are a mist that appears for a little while and then vanishes."
  • Delay that feels short and is not. Augustine writes that his answer to the call was only "Presently, lo, presently; Leave me a little while. But presently, presently, had no present; and my leave me a little while went on for a long while" (Confessions 8.5).
