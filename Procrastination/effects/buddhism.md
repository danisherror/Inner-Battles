The Buddhist texts describe the effects as lost livelihood, a hard life and a wasted chance.

- **Lost income.** After the list of excuses, DN 31 says: "By dwelling on so many excuses for not working, you don't make any more money, and the money you already have runs out." The verses add: "When the young neglect their work like this, riches pass them by."
- **A good deed left undone.** Dhp 314: "A bad deed is better left undone, for it will plague you later on. A good deed is better done, one that does not plague you."
- **A lax effort stirs up dust.** Dhp 313 speaks of "the life gone forth when laxly led", which "just stirs up dust all the more". The verse is about the monastic life.
- **No bargaining with time.** MN 131: "who knows, tomorrow may bring death! For there is no bargain to be struck with Death and his mighty horde."
