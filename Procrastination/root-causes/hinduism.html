Procrastination

Putting off what matters until later, though you know the delay will cost you.

Root causes

The Gita traces the habit of putting things off to ignorance, attachment and the pull of comfort.

  • Ignorance. Verse 14.8 says tamas is "born of ignorance". Verse 14.17 adds: "from the mode of ignorance arise negligence and delusion."
  • Comfort mistaken for happiness. Verse 18.39 describes the happiness of tamas as the kind "derived from sleep, indolence, and negligence". It is pleasant now, which is the same pull that makes delay attractive.
  • Fear of the outcome. Verse 2.47 says: "You have a right to perform your prescribed duties, but you are not entitled to the fruits of your actions. Never consider yourself to be the cause of the results of your activities, nor be attached to inaction." One reading is that worry about results can freeze a person into inaction, which is why the verse names both attachment to fruits and attachment to inaction.
  • The mind as its own enemy. Verse 6.5 says the mind "can be the friend and also the enemy of the self".
