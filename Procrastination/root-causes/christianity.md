Christian writers find the roots of delay in a divided will, in the force of habit, and in fear.

- **A divided will.** In Romans 7:15-19 Paul writes: "I do not understand my own actions. For I do not do what I want, but I do the very thing I hate... For I do not do the good I want, but the evil I do not want is what I do."
- **The force of habit.** Augustine describes how he kept putting off a change he knew he needed. In the *Confessions* (8.5) he says: "the law of sin is the violence of custom, whereby the mind is drawn and held, even against its will; deserving to be so held in that it so willingly falls into it."
- **Waiting for perfect conditions.** Ecclesiastes 11:4: "Whoever observes the wind will not sow, and whoever regards the clouds will not reap."
- **Fear.** In the parable of the talents, the third servant says: "I was afraid, and I went and hid your talent in the ground" (Matthew 25:25).
