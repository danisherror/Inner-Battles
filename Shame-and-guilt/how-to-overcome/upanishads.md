The Upanishads answer with knowledge, and not with a technique. Taittiriya 2.9: "He who thus knows these two (good and bad), frees himself. He who knows both, frees himself."

In Chandogya 7.1.3 Narada asks: "Do, Sir, help me over this grief of mine." The first reply is: "Whatever you have read, is only a name."

This is related to shame and guilt and not identical to them. Neither passage turns into steps, and the teaching that follows Sanatkumara's reply was not read here. The site makes no claim about how to practise it, and nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist.
