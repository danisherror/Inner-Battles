Root causes

The sources read here point to a few causes, and say little about how they work.

  • Early harm. The abstract of the Tangney review lists research on "the link between childhood abuse and later proneness to shame", and on "styles of coping with shame". It does not give the strength of the link or its mechanism.
  • Doing harm. Guilt follows an act: breaking a rule you hold, or hurting someone. That is a standard account and not drawn from the papers.
  • High standards and harsh judging. Many people describe the same pattern as in perfectionism and self-criticism: a standard set too high, then a verdict on the self when it is missed. That is common description, not a finding here.
  • Secrecy. What is hidden tends to grow, as people commonly describe it.

Shame that began in childhood harm is not a personal failing, and a therapist can help with it.
