Effects on life

Psalm 32 describes what hidden guilt does to a body: "While I kept silent, my body wasted away through my groaning all day long. For day and night your hand was heavy upon me; my strength was dried up as by the heat of summer."

Then it turns: "Then I acknowledged my sin to you, and I did not hide my iniquity; I said, 'I will confess my transgressions to the Lord,' and you forgave the guilt of my sin."

Genesis 3:8 shows the hiding that comes with it: the man and woman "hid themselves from the presence of the Lord God".

The Psalm connects silence with wasting and confession with forgiveness. It does not claim that every ache of conscience has this cause, and a footnote on the page says the Hebrew of verse 4 is uncertain.
