Effects on life

The research read here describes costs and benefits, and mostly for shame. In two studies of undergraduates, Tangney, Wagner and Gramzow report that "Shame-proneness was strongly related to psychological maladjustment in general" and that "shame accounted for substantial variance in depression, above and beyond attributional style." Guilt-proneness was "only moderately related to psychopathology", and the correlations "were ascribable entirely to the shared variance between shame and guilt." These are correlations from questionnaires.

The review by Tangney, Stuewig and Mashek weighs "the relative advantages and disadvantages" of the two emotions, and the abstract does not give its conclusions.

Common effects, as people describe them and not findings from these papers: hiding from people, snapping at them, over-apologising, a tight chest, and sometimes, with guilt, repair and a changed course.

Heavy shame can feed low mood. If shame is heavy or long-lasting, is tied to abuse, or comes with thoughts of harming yourself, please talk to a doctor or therapist, and in an emergency contact your local emergency services.
