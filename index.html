The Seven Sins

Understand the habits and struggles that hold us back through six lenses (Modern, Hinduism, Upanishads, Christianity, Buddhism and Philosophical) and learn how to leave them behind.

The seven deadly sins

The classic list, read through each lens.

Pride

Valuing yourself above truth, and above other people.

Greed

Wanting more than you need, and never feeling you have enough.

Lust

Desire that overrides respect, commitment and judgment.

Envy

Pain at another person's good fortune.

Gluttony

Over-indulgence, taking more than you need.

Wrath

Uncontrolled anger, resentment and the wish to get even.

Sloth

Neglecting what matters, from laziness, apathy or avoidance.

Inner battles

Not sins, but other struggles where the opponent is our own mind, explored through the same lenses.

Procrastination

Putting off what matters until later, though you know the delay will cost you.

Overthinking

Going round the same thoughts about the past or the future, unable to settle or to act.

Perfectionism

Holding yourself to standards so high that starting, finishing or being seen feels unsafe.

Self-doubt

Doubting your ability or worth, and feeling like a fraud despite what you have done.

Self-criticism

A harsh inner voice that punishes mistakes instead of helping you improve.

Jealousy

The fear of losing someone or something to a rival, and the hurt and suspicion that follow.

Fear and anxiety

The mind sounding the alarm about threats that have not happened, or are far bigger in the imagination.
A read-only guide for self-improvement. Educational and reflective content, not medical, psychological or religious authority. If a habit is harming your life, please talk to a doctor, therapist or a trusted person.
