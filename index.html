      The seven deadly sins

      7 topics

      The classic list, read through each lens.

      Envy

      Pain at another person's good fortune.

      Gluttony

      Over-indulgence, taking more than you need.

      Greed

      Wanting more than you need, and never feeling you have enough.

      Lust

      Desire that overrides respect, commitment and judgment.

      Pride

      Valuing yourself above truth, and above other people.

      Sloth

      Neglecting what matters, from laziness, apathy or avoidance.

      Wrath

      Uncontrolled anger, resentment and the wish to get even.

      Inner battles

      19 topics

      Not sins, but other struggles where the opponent is our own mind, explored through the same lenses.

      Addiction and compulsion

      The pull toward something you have decided to stop.

      Burnout and apathy

      Exhaustion that flattens motivation.

      Delusion and the true self

      Mistaking the passing parts of you for the whole of who you are.

      Denial and self-deception

      Not letting yourself see the truth, and explaining away the discomfort that follows.

      Distraction

      A mind pulled in many directions at once, unable to stay with what matters.

      Ego and validation

      Needing approval and praise to feel worthy.

      Fear and anxiety

      The mind sounding the alarm about threats that have not happened, or are far bigger in the imagination.

      Fear of failure and success

      Staying out of the attempt because you dread falling short, or dread what succeeding would bring.

      Hopelessness and despair

      Believing that nothing will change, and that nothing you do will matter.

      Impulsivity

      Acting on the urge of the moment and giving up a larger, later good for a smaller, sooner one.

      Jealousy

      The fear of losing someone or something to a rival, and the hurt and suspicion that follow.

      Loneliness and isolation

      The urge to withdraw even while wanting connection.

      Overthinking

      Going round the same thoughts about the past or the future, unable to settle or to act.

      Perfectionism

      Holding yourself to standards so high that starting, finishing or being seen feels unsafe.

      Procrastination

      Putting off what matters until later, though you know the delay will cost you.

      Resentment

      Holding on to an old hurt, so the past keeps paying rent in your head.

      Self-criticism

      A harsh inner voice that punishes mistakes instead of helping you improve.

      Self-doubt

      Doubting your ability or worth, and feeling like a fraud despite what you have done.

      Shame and guilt

      Feeling bad about what you did, or about who you are, and what to do with it.
