The Upanishads do not name jealousy, and the closest they come is a warning against grasping at what belongs to another. Isha Upanishad 1: "All this, whatsoever moves on earth, is to be hidden in the Lord (the Self). When thou hast surrendered all this, then thou mayest enjoy. Do not covet the wealth of any man!"

The verse says to give up the sense of owning and then enjoy. It does not say to stop loving.

This is related to jealousy and not identical to it. The verse is about coveting wealth, which is closer to greed and envy than to the fear of losing someone to a rival. Reading it as speaking to jealousy is ours.
