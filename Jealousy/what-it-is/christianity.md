Christian texts speak of jealousy as a force that is hard to stand against. Proverbs 27:4: "Wrath is cruel, anger is overwhelming, but who is able to stand before jealousy?"

The Song of Solomon links it with love. In the King James Version, 8:6 reads: "love is strong as death; jealousy is cruel as the grave". The NRSV renders the same line "passion fierce as the grave".

The Bible also calls God "a jealous God" (Exodus 20:5). That is a statement about God's claim on his people, and this article is about human jealousy, so it is not treated here.

This is related to jealousy and not identical to it. Proverbs and the Song of Solomon speak of jealousy as a power, and do not define it as the fear of losing someone to a rival.
