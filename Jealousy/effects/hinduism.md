The Gita traces where an unchecked mind ends. Gita 2.63: "Anger leads to clouding of judgment, which results in bewilderment of memory. When memory is bewildered, the intellect gets destroyed; and when the intellect is destroyed, one is ruined."

The translator's commentary on 6.6 says of the enemies of the mind: "the demons sitting within our own mind have the ability to make us live in constant wretchedness". It lists "lust, anger, greed, envy, illusion" among them.

By contrast, Gita 12.15 describes the one who is "not a source of annoyance to anyone and who in turn are not agitated by anyone".

This is related to jealousy and not identical to it. The verses do not mention jealousy, and the commentary names envy and not jealousy. Reading the chain as the toll of jealousy is ours.
