The Gita describes where *tamas* leads.

- **Darkness and delusion.** Gita 14.13: when *tamas* prevails, there is darkness, inertia, heedlessness and delusion.
- **Downward path.** Gita 14.18: those in *tamas* go downward. 14.16-17 say that the fruit of *tamas* is ignorance, and that ignorance and delusion arise from it.
- **Hollow happiness.** Gita 18.39: the happiness of *tamas* deludes from beginning to end, arising from sleep, sloth and heedlessness.
- **Duty undone.** Gita 3.8 and 3.20 stress that action is better than inaction and that even keeping the body going depends on action. 3.16 says one who does not turn the wheel of action "lives in vain."
- **Obstacle to yoga.** Yoga Sutras 1.30-31: laziness is an obstacle that brings distress.
