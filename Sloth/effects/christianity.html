Effects on life

Scripture and tradition list sloth's costs.

  • Poverty. Proverbs 6:9-11: "A little sleep, a little slumber... and poverty will come upon you like a robber." Proverbs 10:4: "A slack hand causes poverty, but the hand of the diligent makes rich."
  • Unfinished work. Proverbs 12:24 says diligent hands will rule, while the lazy will be put to forced labour.
  • A buried talent. In Matthew 25:24-30, the servant who hid his master's money out of fear loses what he had.
  • Burden on others. 2 Thessalonians 3:10-12 asks those who live in idleness to earn their own bread.
  • Despair. The desert fathers saw acedia as leading to despair and abandoning the call of one's life.
  • Neglected love. Matthew 25:41-45 shows judgment on those who did nothing for the hungry, strangers and sick.

These verses are about those able but unwilling to work, and do not condemn people who cannot. The description of acedia ("noonday demon", fourth to eighth hour, the day seeming long, hatred of the place and manual work, desire for other places) was read in Luke Dysinger's translation of Evagrius (earlychurchtexts.com (external link)) on 2026-11-14; that page gives no chapter number, so the Praktikos 12 reference rests on secondary sources (for example the Wikipedia outline of the Praktikos) and was not checked in a printed edition.
