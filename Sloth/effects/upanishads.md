The Upanishads describe the cost of heedlessness as missing the goal.

- **Missing the Self.** Mundaka 3.2.4: the Self cannot be gained by the heedless or the weak.
- **Staying asleep.** Katha 1.3.14: "Arise, awake!" The cost of not waking is staying on the wrong path.
- **Wandering.** Katha 1.2.5-6: the ignorant go round and round, and the one who thinks "this world is all there is" falls under death's sway again and again.
- **A wasted chance.** Kena 2.5 says that if one knows here, then there is truth; if one does not know here, there is great loss. The human birth is the chance to know.
- **Neglected duties.** Taittiriya 1.11: do not be heedless about study, teaching, truth and dharma.
