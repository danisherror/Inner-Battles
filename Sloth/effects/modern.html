Effects on life

Chronic avoidance costs more than the task you put off.

  • Stress and guilt. Procrastinators report higher stress and worse well-being on average, as the work and the worry build up.
  • Health. Studies link procrastination with poorer sleep and delay in seeking medical care.
  • Work and money. Missed deadlines, lower performance and late bills.
  • Relationships. Others carry what you leave undone, which breeds resentment.
  • Lost growth. Skills and goals stall, and regret about unlived years is common.
  • Spiral. Shame about avoiding makes the next task feel worse, which feeds more avoidance.
