The Pali words are **thina-middha**, sloth and torpor, the third of the five hindrances to meditation, and **kosajja**, laziness, whose opposite is **viriya**, energy or effort.

*Thina* is sluggishness of mind and *middha* is heaviness or drowsiness. They are described as a mental state, which can have causes such as heavy eating, and can be dealt with.

The Buddha's last words, in the *Mahaparinibbana Sutta* (DN 16), were: "All conditioned things are subject to decay; strive on with diligence (*appamadena sampadetha*)."

Dhammapada 280 says that one who does not rise when it is time to rise, and who is young and strong but given to idleness, with a mind full of sluggish thoughts, will not find the way by wisdom.
