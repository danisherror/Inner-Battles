Hinduism has no single word equal to sloth, but the closest ideas are **alasya** (laziness) and **tamas**, the quality of inertia, dullness and heaviness, one of the three *gunas*.

The Gita describes *tamas* in several places. In 14.8, it is born of ignorance and binds through heedlessness (*pramada*), laziness (*alasya*) and sleep (*nidra*). In 18.28, a *tamasic* doer is "unsteady, vulgar, stubborn, deceitful, malicious, lazy (*alasa*), despondent and procrastinating (*dirgha-sutri*)." And 18.39 describes a *tamasic* happiness that comes from sleep, laziness and heedlessness.

Yoga Sutras 1.30 names *alasya* (laziness, heaviness of body and mind) among the nine obstacles to yoga.

This is related to sloth and not identical to it: Hinduism sees it as a guna to be balanced, not as a sin.
