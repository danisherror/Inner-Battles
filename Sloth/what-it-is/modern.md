Sloth is neglecting what matters, out of laziness, apathy or avoidance. It is not the same as resting. Rest restores you. Sloth is putting off or skipping the things you know you should do for yourself and others, until the neglect becomes a habit.

Modern terms for the pieces are:

- **Procrastination**, delaying a task despite expecting to be worse off for it.
- **Avoidance**, steering away from things that cause discomfort.
- **Apathy**, a loss of care or motivation.
- **Learned helplessness**, giving up because past effort seemed pointless.

Sloth also differs from **depression**, which is a medical condition that can look like laziness (low energy, no motivation) but is not a moral failing.
