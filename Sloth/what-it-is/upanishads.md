The Upanishads do not list laziness as a sin. They treat it as the opposite of the effort the path demands, and as a form of the heedlessness (*pramada*) that keeps the seeker asleep.

- **Wake up.** Katha 1.3.14 says: "Arise, awake! Having obtained your boons, understand them. The path is sharp as a razor's edge, hard to cross, say the wise."
- **Strength needed.** Mundaka 3.2.4 says "This Self cannot be gained by one without strength, nor through heedlessness (*pramada*), nor by austerity without the right aim."
- **Neglect of duty to learn and teach.** Taittiriya 1.11 tells the graduate not to neglect study and teaching (*svadhyaya-pravacanabhyam na pramaditavyam*), nor truth, dharma, welfare, prosperity or duties to gods and ancestors.

The theme is related to sloth and not identical to it: the texts speak of heedlessness, not of a sin of laziness.
