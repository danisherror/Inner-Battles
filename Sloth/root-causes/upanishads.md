The Upanishads place the cause in **heedlessness (pramada) and ignorance**.

- **Heedlessness.** Mundaka 3.2.4 names *pramada* as one of the ways the Self is missed.
- **Ignorance as sleep.** Katha 1.3.14 says "Arise, awake," which implies that ordinary life is a kind of sleep.
- **Choosing the pleasant.** Katha 1.2.1-2: the unwise choose the pleasant (*preyas*), which includes ease and comfort, over the good (*shreyas*).
- **Not seeing the aim.** Katha 1.2.5: living in ignorance, people go round like the blind led by the blind. Without a goal, there is nothing to rouse effort.
- **Lack of strength.** Mundaka 3.2.4 says the Self is not gained by one lacking strength (*balahina*), which links effort to inner vigour.
