Root causes

Sloth rarely comes from simple "laziness". Common roots are:

  • Avoiding discomfort. Tasks that cause anxiety, boredom or fear of failure get postponed to feel better now.
  • Present bias. Humans tend to prefer immediate reward over later benefit, so future costs feel small.
  • Perfectionism and fear of failure. If you might not do it well, not starting protects your self-image.
  • Unclear or overwhelming goals. A big, vague task is hard to begin.
  • Low energy. Poor sleep, inactivity, diet, and mental-health conditions such as depression reduce drive.
  • Distraction. Phones and feeds offer instant, easy rewards.
  • Learned helplessness. After repeated failure or lack of control, people stop trying.
