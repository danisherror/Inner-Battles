Root causes

The Gita gives a clear chain. In 2.62-63 Krishna describes how it begins: dwelling on the objects of the senses creates attachment, attachment gives rise to desire, desire to anger, anger to delusion, delusion to loss of memory, and loss of memory to the destruction of discernment, so that the person is ruined.

Two sources are named:

  • Rajas. In 3.37 desire is said to be born of rajas, the restless, passionate quality of nature. A rajasic mind is always reaching for the next thing.
  • The unsteady mind and senses. In 6.35 Arjuna admits the mind is restless and hard to restrain, and Krishna replies that it can be controlled by practice (abhyasa) and detachment (vairagya).

Notice where the chain starts: not with the body, but with what the mind keeps dwelling on. That is why the tradition puts so much weight on what you look at, hear and keep company with.
