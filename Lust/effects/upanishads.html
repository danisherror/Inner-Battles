Effects on life

The Upanishads describe the effects in terms of where desire takes you.

  • Destiny follows desire. According to the Brihadaranyaka (4.4.5), as a person's desire is, so is their will; as their will, so their deed; as their deed, so what they become. A life led by lust is steered toward what lust leads to.
  • Wandering senses. In the Katha chariot image, the undisciplined person's senses run like unmanageable horses (1.3.5), so they are carried wherever the horses go.
  • Missing the good. Choosing the pleasant over the good means losing what is of lasting value (Katha 1.2).
  • Staying in the cycle. The tradition holds that those caught up in desire remain bound to repeated birth and death, while the one free from desire is freed (a theme in the Brihadaranyaka and Katha).

In short, the cost is loss of direction and of freedom, and in the long view, of liberation (moksha).
