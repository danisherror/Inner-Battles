Christianity lens

Effects on life

The Bible speaks of the effects of lust in terms of damage to oneself, to others and to one's relationship with God.

  • Death as the end of the path. James 1:15 says that sin, when it is full-grown, brings forth death, and the sequence begins with desire.
  • Harm to one's own body. Paul says sexual sin is a sin against one's own body, which he calls a temple of the Holy Spirit (1 Corinthians 6:18-19).
  • Harm to others. Using a person as an object wrongs them. The Catechism says pornography "perverts the conjugal act, the intimate giving of spouses to each other" and does grave injury to the dignity of those involved (2354).
  • Exclusion from the good life. Galatians 5:19-21 lists sexual immorality among works of the flesh and contrasts them with the fruit of the Spirit (5:22-23): love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness and self-control.
  • The seriousness of the heart. Jesus uses strong language in Matthew 5:29-30, meaning that what feeds sin should be cut away at whatever cost.

Christianity also keeps open the way back: the same Scriptures stress forgiveness and a new start for those who turn from sin.
