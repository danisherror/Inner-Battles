When lust runs a person's choices, the costs tend to show up in four places.

- **Relationships.** Trust breaks when desire leads to secrecy, infidelity or treating a partner as a means to an end. Even without a breach, constant objectifying can make real intimacy feel flat.
- **Time and focus.** Hours go to chasing or consuming, and attention for work, study and people shrinks.
- **Mood and self-image.** The pattern of promise, lapse and regret feeds shame, which tends to make the cycle worse rather than better.
- **Real-world harm.** In more serious cases it leads to risk-taking, financial cost, or harm to other people.

The ICD-11 entry for compulsive sexual behaviour disorder names this directly: it requires marked distress or significant impairment in personal, family, social, educational or occupational life. If you recognise that level of impact, speaking to a doctor or therapist is a sound step, not a failure.
