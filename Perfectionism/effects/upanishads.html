Perfectionism

Holding yourself to standards so high that starting, finishing or being seen feels unsafe.

Effects on life

The Upanishads do not list the effects of perfectionism. Two passages show what a life tied to achievement can lack, and what is said to ease it.

  • Sternness and conceit. Svetaketu comes home from his studies "conceited, considering himself well-read, and stern" (Chandogya 6.1.2). His father's question shows that all his learning has left something out.
  • Sorrow and its end. Isha 7: "When to a man who understands, the Self has become all things, what sorrow, what trouble can there be to him who once beheld that unity?" The Upanishad ties the end of sorrow to seeing unity, and says nothing here about performance or standards.

This is related to perfectionism and not identical to it. Neither passage is about perfectionism, and the effects above are drawn by reading them for what they say about grief and attainment. That reading is ours.
