Perfectionism

Holding yourself to standards so high that starting, finishing or being seen feels unsafe.

Effects on life

The harp image in AN 6.55 names the cost of driving too hard.

  • Restlessness. The Buddha tells Soṇa: "when energy is too forceful it leads to restlessness. When energy is too slack it leads to laziness." Strain does not produce calm; it produces the agitation the practice is meant to settle.
  • Wanting to quit. Soṇa, who has driven himself hard and not seen the result he wants, thinks of resigning the training and going back to "a lesser life". Over-striving that does not pay off can end in giving up.
  • Two ways to miss. The harp is "not resonant and playable" if strung too tight or too slack, so the text says that too much effort is as much of a problem as too little.

This is related to perfectionism and not identical to it. The sutta concerns effort in meditation, and the match to perfectionism is ours.
