Perfectionism

How to overcome it

The Isha Upanishad offers a short way of acting without clinging.

  1. Surrender the result. Verse 1: "All this, whatsoever moves on earth, is to be hidden in the Lord (the Self). When thou hast surrendered all this, then thou mayest enjoy."
  2. Keep acting. Verse 2: "Though a man may wish to live a hundred years, performing works, it will be thus with him; but not in any other way: work will thus not cling to a man."
  3. See the one Self in everything. Verses 6-7: "he never turns away from it", and "what sorrow, what trouble can there be to him who once beheld that unity?"

Müller's note says the verse allows continued work "provided they are performed without any selfish motives, without any desire of reward". He also reports that Śaṅkara read verse 2 differently, so the meaning is disputed.

This is related to perfectionism and not identical to it. The Upanishad does not discuss standards or fear of mistakes, and using it for perfectionism is a reading.

Sources
