The advice of AN 6.55 is to tune the effort, as a harp is tuned.

1. **Neither too tight nor too slack.** The Buddha asks Soṇa about strings "tuned neither too tight nor too slack, but fixed at an even tension" and whether the harp then sounded well. It did.
2. **Balance energy and serenity.** "So, Soṇa, you should focus on energy and serenity, find a balance of the faculties, and learn the character of this situation."
3. **Look at the result.** The sutta reports that Soṇa did this, and then, "living alone, withdrawn, diligent, keen, and resolute, soon realized the supreme culmination of the spiritual path". The effort stayed; the strain went.
4. **Avoid both extremes.** SN 56.11 describes a "middle way of practice" that avoids "indulgence in sensual pleasures" and "indulgence in self-mortification". The extremes there are not about perfectionism, but the pattern of avoiding both is the same.

This is related to perfectionism and not identical to it. The suttas give guidance for practice, and applying the harp to everyday standards is a reading.
