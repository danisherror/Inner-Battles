The Christian texts offer grace, honesty about weakness and balance.

1. **Receive grace.** Ephesians 2:8-9: "For by grace you have been saved through faith, and this is not your own doing; it is the gift of God—not the result of works, so that no one may boast."
2. **Remember compassion.** Psalm 103:13-14: "he knows how we were made; he remembers that we are dust."
3. **Admit weakness.** 2 Corinthians 12:9: "My grace is sufficient for you, for power is made perfect in weakness."
4. **Hold both.** Ecclesiastes 7:16-18: "Do not be too righteous, and do not act too wise; why should you destroy yourself? ... It is good that you should take hold of the one without letting go of the other". The passage warns against both extremes.
5. **Press on without being finished.** Philippians 3:13-14: "forgetting what lies behind and straining forward to what lies ahead, I press on toward the goal".

This is related to perfectionism and not identical to it. Christian counsel for a person weighed down by guilt or anxiety also includes pastoral and medical help, which these verses do not discuss.
