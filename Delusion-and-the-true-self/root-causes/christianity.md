The New Testament names ignorance and a hardened heart. Ephesians 4:17-18: "you must no longer walk as the gentiles walk, in the futility of their minds; they are darkened in their understanding, alienated from the life of God because of their ignorance and hardness of heart."

This is related to the idea of the true self and not identical to it. The passage is about darkened understanding and a way of life, and does not speak of ignorance of the self. The wording in the page's footnote on "gentiles" was not read. Reading the verses as a root of ignorance of oneself is ours.
