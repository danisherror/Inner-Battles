Delusion and the true self

Mistaking the passing parts of you for the whole of who you are.

Root causes

The Buddhist texts put ignorance at the start of the chain that ends in suffering. SN 12.1: "Ignorance is a requirement for choices. Choices are a requirement for consciousness."

The sutta continues through craving and grasping to its end: "That is how this entire mass of suffering originates."

This is related to the idea of the true self and not identical to it. SN 12.1 is about dependent origination, and the ignorance it names is the sutta's own term, which this article does not claim is ignorance of a true self. This topic's title does not fit the Buddhist texts, which teach not-self.
