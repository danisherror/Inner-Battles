The Katha Upanishad says what unmastered senses do. Katha 1.3.5: "He who has no understanding and whose mind (the reins) is never firmly held, his senses (horses) are unmanageable, like vicious horses of a charioteer."

It gives the opposite in 1.2.22: "The wise who knows the Self as bodiless within the bodies, as unchanging among changing things, as great and omnipresent, does never grieve."

This is related to the idea of the true self and not identical to it. The first verse is about understanding and the senses, and the second about the wise who knows the Self. Reading the first as the cost of ignorance of the Self is ours.
