Delusion and the true self

Mistaking the passing parts of you for the whole of who you are.

Effects on life

The Gita says what the illusion costs. Gita 7.27: "the dualities of desire and aversion arise from illusion."

And at the start of the teaching, Krishna says: "While you speak words of wisdom, you are mourning for that which is not worthy of grief" (2.11).

This is related to the idea of the true self and not identical to it. 7.27 is about desire and aversion, and 2.11 is addressed to Arjuna in a battlefield. Reading them as the cost of ignorance of the self is ours.
