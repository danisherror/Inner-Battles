Delusion and the true self

Mistaking the passing parts of you for the whole of who you are.

Effects on life

The Buddhist texts say what taking the changing for a self brings. SN 22.59: "But because form is not-self, it leads to affliction. And you can't compel form: 'May my form be like this! May it not be like that!'"

SN 12.1 gives the whole chain: "Rebirth is a requirement for old age and death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, sadness, and distress to come to be."

This is related to the idea of the true self and not identical to it. SN 22.59 is about the five aggregates and SN 12.1 about dependent origination, and both speak of not-self and not of a true self. Reading them as the cost of mistaking what the self is is ours.
