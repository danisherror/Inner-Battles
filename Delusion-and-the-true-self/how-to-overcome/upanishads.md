The Upanishads point to knowing the Self, and to guiding the chariot.

1. **Know the Self in the chariot.** Katha 1.3.3: "Know the Self to be sitting in the chariot, the body to be the chariot, the intellect (buddhi) the charioteer, and the mind the reins."
2. **Hold the reins.** Katha 1.3.9: "But he who has understanding for his charioteer, and who holds the reins of the mind, he reaches the end of his journey".
3. **Hear the teaching.** Chandogya 6.8.7: "It is the True. It is the Self, and thou ... art it."
4. **Do not grieve.** Katha 1.2.22: "The wise who knows the Self as bodiless within the bodies, as unchanging among changing things, as great and omnipresent, does never grieve."

This is related to the idea of the true self and not identical to it. These verses are about the Self, the chariot and a teaching to a student. Reading them as a way through ignorance of the self is ours, and nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist.
