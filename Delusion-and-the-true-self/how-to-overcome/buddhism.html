How to overcome it

The Buddhist texts counsel seeing the five aggregates for what they are.

  1. Ask the question. SN 22.59: "is it fit to be regarded thus: 'This is mine, I am this, this is my self'?" "No, sir."
  2. See with right understanding. SN 22.59: "This is not mine, I am not this, this is not my self."
  3. See it with wisdom. Dhp 279: "All things are not-self—when this is seen with wisdom, one grows disillusioned with suffering: this is the path to purity."
  4. Let ignorance fade. SN 12.1: "When ignorance fades away and ceases no residue left behind, choices cease."

This is related to the idea of the true self and not identical to it. These passages are about the five aggregates and not-self, and they sit within the Buddhist path. The texts do not say that a person does not matter, and nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist.
