What it is

The Upanishads name ignorance and place it against wisdom. Katha 1.2.4: "Wide apart and leading to different points are these two, ignorance, and what is known as wisdom."

And Chandogya 6.8.7, a father to his son: "Now that which is that subtile essence (the root of all), in it all that exists has its self. It is the True. It is the Self, and thou ... art it."

This is related to the idea of the true self and not identical to it. The Katha verse sets ignorance against wisdom, and the Chandogya verse is a teaching to one student about the Self.
