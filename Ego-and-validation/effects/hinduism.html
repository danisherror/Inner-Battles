Effects on life

The Gita says what ego-driven action costs. Gita 17.18: "Austerity that is performed with ostentation for the sake of gaining honor, respect, and adoration is in the mode of passion. Its benefits are unstable and transitory."

Krishna also warns Arjuna: "But if, due to pride, you do not listen to My advice, you will perish" (18.58).

This is related to the need for validation and not identical to it. 17.18 is about one kind of austerity, and 18.58 is a warning to one person about pride. Reading them as the cost of seeking approval is ours.
