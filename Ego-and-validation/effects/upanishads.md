The Upanishads name the cost of choosing the pleasant. Katha 1.2.1: "It is well with him who clings to the good; he who chooses the pleasant, misses his end."

Mundaka 3.1.2 describes the grief: "On the same tree man sits grieving, immersed, bewildered by his own impotence."

This is related to the need for validation and not identical to it. The verses are about the good and the pleasant and about the self and the other lord, and do not mention approval. Reading them as the toll of living on applause is ours.
