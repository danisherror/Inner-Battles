The Bible's counsel is to do good quietly and to look to a different judge.

1. **Give in secret.** Matthew 6:3-4: "But when you give alms, do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing, so that your alms may be done in secret, and your Father who sees in secret will reward you."
2. **Pray unseen.** Matthew 6:6: "But whenever you pray, go into your room and shut the door and pray to your Father who is in secret".
3. **Serve.** Matthew 23:11-12: "The greatest among you will be your servant. All who exalt themselves will be humbled, and all who humble themselves will be exalted."
4. **Ask whom you are trying to please.** Galatians 1:10: "Am I now seeking human approval or God's approval? Or am I trying to please people? If I were still pleasing people, I would not be a servant of Christ."

This is related to the need for validation and not identical to it. These verses are about giving, prayer and service, and they do not tell anyone to avoid all company or praise. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist.
