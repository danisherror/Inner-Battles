**Arjuna and the pride that decides**

The Gita has no tale of a person who needs praise, so this is a moment in the dialogue and not a story of validation. Near the end Krishna warns Arjuna: "If you always remember Me, by My grace you shall overcome all obstacles and difficulties. But if, due to pride, you do not listen to My advice, you will perish." And: "If, motivated by pride, you think, 'I shall not fight,' your decision will be in vain. Your own nature will compel you to fight" (18.58-59).

He goes on: "that action which out of delusion you do not wish to do, you will be driven to do it by your own inclination, born of your own material nature" (18.60).

Arjuna answers in 18.73: "O Infallible One, by Your grace my illusion has been dispelled, and I am situated in knowledge. I am now free from doubts, and I shall act according to Your instructions."

**What it shows:** A pride that decides for you can be set down, and Arjuna says so himself. The passage is about pride, duty and fighting and not about approval; the link is ours, and Arjuna's reply is about the whole teaching and not only about pride.
