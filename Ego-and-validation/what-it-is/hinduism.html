Hinduism lens

What it is

Hinduism has no single word in the Bhagavad Gita for needing approval, but it names the ego-sense behind it. Gita 3.27: "All activities are carried out by the three modes of material nature. But in ignorance, the soul, deluded by false identification with the body, thinks of itself as the doer."

The page's word-by-word gloss renders the compound for "deluded" as "those who are bewildered by the ego". A self that believes it is the doer will also want credit for what is done.

This is related to the need for validation and not identical to it. The verse is about doership and false identification, and does not mention praise. The step from doer to wanting credit is ours.
