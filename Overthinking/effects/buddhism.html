Overthinking

Going round the same thoughts about the past or the future, unable to settle or to act.

Effects on life

The Buddhist texts describe the effects as added suffering, a growing habit and a tired mind.

  • A second arrow. SN 36.6: the unlearned person "sorrow[s] and wail[s] and lament[s], beating their breast and falling into confusion" and so feels both the physical pain and the mental pain, like being struck by a second arrow.
  • Restlessness grows with attention. SN 46.51: frequent irrational attention to an unsettled mind "makes them increase and grow".
  • The inclination deepens. MN 19: what a person frequently thinks about becomes the inclination of their mind.
  • Even good thoughts can tire. In MN 19 the Buddha says of wholesome thoughts: "thinking and considering for too long would tire my body. And when the body is tired, the mind is stressed. And when the mind is stressed, it's far from immersion."
