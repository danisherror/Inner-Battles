Overthinking

Going round the same thoughts about the past or the future, unable to settle or to act.

How to overcome it

The Gita's answer is practice, detachment and returning again and again.

  • Practice and detachment. Krishna replies to Arjuna in 6.35: "the mind is indeed very difficult to restrain. But by practice and detachment, it can be controlled."
  • Bring it back. Verse 6.26: "Whenever and wherever the restless and unsteady mind wanders, one should bring it back and continually focus it on God." The instruction expects the mind to wander and says what to do when it does. In this translation the object is God; reading it as "your chosen object of attention" is our gloss.
  • Be like the ocean. Verse 2.70: the sage remains undisturbed, as the ocean is by the rivers that flow into it, and attains peace.
  • Lift yourself. Verse 6.5: "Elevate yourself through the power of your mind, and not degrade yourself, for the mind can be the friend and also the enemy of the self."
