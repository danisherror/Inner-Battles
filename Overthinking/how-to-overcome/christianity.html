Inner battle

Overthinking

Going round the same thoughts about the past or the future, unable to settle or to act.

How to overcome it

The Christian answer is to hand the worry over, to speak to the soul, and to guard the mind.

  • Hand it over. Philippians 4:6-7: "Do not be anxious about anything, but in everything by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known to God. And the peace of God, which surpasses all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus." 1 Peter 5:7: "Cast all your anxiety on him, because he cares for you."
  • Take one day at a time. Matthew 6:34: "Today's trouble is enough for today."
  • Talk to your own soul. Psalm 42:5 answers its own question: "Hope in God, for I shall again praise him".
  • Seek a good word. Proverbs 12:25: "a good word cheers it up". Other people can help.
  • Take thoughts captive. 2 Corinthians 10:5: "we take every thought captive to obey Christ."
  • Renew the mind. Romans 12:2: "be transformed by the renewing of the mind".
  • Don't feed what lingers. Evagrius: whether thoughts linger "is up to us."
  • Steadfast mind. Isaiah 26:3: "Those of steadfast mind you keep in peace".
