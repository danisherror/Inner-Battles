Scripture places the roots of a worried, circling mind in anxiety about life, in being pulled in too many directions, and in thoughts that are allowed to stay.

- **Anxiety about provision and the future.** Jesus names food, drink and clothing, and says: "Therefore do not worry, saying, 'What will we eat?' or 'What will we drink?' or 'What will we wear?'" He calls his hearers "you of little faith" (Matthew 6:30-31).
- **Too many things at once.** Luke 10:41: "you are worried and distracted by many things".
- **A disquieted soul.** Psalm 42:5: "Why are you cast down, O my soul, and why are you disquieted within me?"
- **Thoughts allowed to linger.** Evagrius: "whether they linger or not, and whether they arouse passions or not; that is up to us."
