The Upanishads locate the root in a mind that is not held, and in identifying with one's own helplessness.

- **Reins not held.** Katha 1.3.5: "He who has no understanding and whose mind (the reins) is never firmly held, his senses (horses) are unmanageable". The senses and thoughts run where they like.
- **Being immersed in one's own distress.** Mundaka 3.1.2: "On the same tree man sits grieving, immersed, bewildered by his own impotence." The man eats the fruit and is caught in it, while the other bird, in 3.1.1, "looks on without eating".
- **Unmindfulness.** Katha 1.3.7: "He who has no understanding, who is unmindful and always impure, never reaches that place, but enters into the round of births."
