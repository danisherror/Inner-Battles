How to overcome it

The Gita's way is to make the mind a friend through discipline, and then to put down the weight.

  1. Elevate, do not degrade. 6.5: "Elevate yourself through the power of your mind, and not degrade yourself, for the mind can be the friend and also the enemy of the self."
  2. Train the mind. 6.6: "For those who have conquered the mind, it is their friend."
  3. Do not fear. 18.66: "Abandon all varieties of dharmas and simply surrender unto Me alone. I shall liberate you from all sinful reactions; do not fear."
  4. Trust the resolve. 9.30-31: even "the vilest sinners" who worship with exclusive devotion "are to be considered righteous because they have made the proper resolve", and "no devotee of Mine is ever lost".

This is related to self-criticism and not identical to it. The verses are about devotion and the mind, and nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist for relentless self-attack.
