How to overcome it

The Bible's answer to the condemning heart is to point to a greater one.

  1. Remember who is greater. 1 John 3:20: "whenever our hearts condemn us, for God is greater than our hearts, and he knows everything."
  2. Receive the verdict. Romans 8:1: "there is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus."
  3. Remember how far. Psalm 103:12: "as far as the east is from the west, so far he removes our transgressions from us."
  4. Go home. In Luke 15, the son gets up and returns, though his words are about unworthiness. The father is already running to meet him (15:20).
  5. Look at your own eye first, kindly. Matthew 7:5 asks for honesty about oneself before judging others; it is not a call for self-attack.

This is related to self-criticism and not identical to it. Christian care for a person weighed down by guilt also includes pastoral and medical help, which these verses do not discuss.
