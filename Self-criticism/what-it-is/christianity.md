Christian texts do not speak of an inner critic, but they speak of the heart that condemns. 1 John 3:19-20: "And by this we will know that we are from the truth and will reassure our hearts before him whenever our hearts condemn us, for God is greater than our hearts, and he knows everything."

And Romans 8:1: "Therefore there is now no condemnation for those who are in Christ Jesus."

This is related to self-criticism and not identical to it. The verses are about conscience and standing before God, and not about a pattern of harsh self-talk. Reading them as speaking to the inner critic is ours.
