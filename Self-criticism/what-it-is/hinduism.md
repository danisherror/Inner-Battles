Hinduism has no single word for the inner critic, but the Bhagavad Gita describes the mind that turns on its owner. 6.5: "Elevate yourself through the power of your mind, and not degrade yourself, for the mind can be the friend and also the enemy of the self." And 6.6: "For those who have conquered the mind, it is their friend. For those who have failed to do so, the mind works like an enemy."

The translator's commentary on 6.5 adds: "We are responsible for our own elevation or debasement."

This is related to self-criticism and not identical to it. The Gita is speaking of the undisciplined mind in general, and does not describe a harsh voice of self-judgement. Reading 6.5-6.6 as speaking to the inner critic is ours.
