Upanishads lens

Effects on life

The Upanishads do not describe the effects of resentment, but they describe grief and bewilderment. The Mundaka Upanishad (3.1.2) says that a person "sits grieving, immersed, bewildered by his own impotence", and that when he sees the other, "then his grief passes away".

This is related to resentment and not identical to it. The passage is about grief and the Self, and does not mention resentment. Reading it as the toll of a grudge is ours.
