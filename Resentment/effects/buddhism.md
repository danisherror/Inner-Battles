The Buddhist texts name what a kept grudge does. Dhp 3: "For those who bear such a grudge, hatred is never laid to rest."

Dhp 1 says what follows corrupt intent: "suffering follows you, like a wheel, the ox's foot." And Dhp 2 gives the opposite: "If with pure intent you speak or act, happiness follows you like a shadow that never leaves."

This is related to resentment and not identical to it. Dhp 1 and 2 are about intention in general. Reading them as the toll and the relief of a grudge is ours.
