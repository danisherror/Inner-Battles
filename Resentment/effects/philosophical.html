Effects on life

Aristotle names the cost of this kind of anger. Nicomachean Ethics 4.5: "Such people are most troublesome to themselves and to their dearest friends."

And he adds: "bad-tempered people are worse to live with."

This is related to resentment and not identical to it. Aristotle is speaking of sulky and bad-tempered people, and his sentences are general observations and not a study of what grudges do. Reading them as the toll of a grudge is ours.
