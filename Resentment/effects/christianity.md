The Bible shows what bitterness and hatred do.

- **Strife.** Proverbs 10:12: "Hatred stirs up strife, but love covers all offenses."
- **Spreading trouble.** Hebrews 12:15: "no root of bitterness springs up and causes trouble and through it many become defiled."
- **A list of the damage.** Ephesians 4:31: "Put away from you all bitterness and wrath and anger and wrangling and slander, together with all malice."

This is related to resentment and not identical to it. These are general statements about hatred and bitterness, and do not describe the toll of a kept grudge on the person who holds it.
