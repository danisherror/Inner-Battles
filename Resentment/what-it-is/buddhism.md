The Dhammapada opens with the grudge. Dhp 3-4: "'They abused me, they hit me! They beat me, they robbed me!' For those who bear such a grudge, hatred is never laid to rest. 'They abused me, they hit me! They beat me, they robbed me!' For those who bear no such grudge, hatred is laid to rest."

This is a direct match for the Buddhist lens, since the translation uses the word "grudge". The verses are about hatred between people, and the Pali terms behind them were not examined.
