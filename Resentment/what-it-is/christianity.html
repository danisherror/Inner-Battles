Christianity lens

What it is

The Bible names the grudge directly. Leviticus 19:17-18: "You shall not hate in your heart anyone of your kin; you shall reprove your neighbor, or you will incur guilt yourself. You shall not take vengeance or bear a grudge against any of your people, but you shall love your neighbor as yourself: I am the Lord."

Joseph's brothers fear one after their father dies: "What if Joseph still bears a grudge against us and pays us back in full for all the wrong that we did to him?" (Genesis 50:15).

This is a direct match for the Christian lens, since the Bible uses the word. Leviticus is addressed to a community, and it also tells the person to "reprove your neighbor", so the verse does not say to say nothing about a wrong.
