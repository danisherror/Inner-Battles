Upanishads lens

What it is

The Upanishads do not name resentment, and the closest they come is a picture of seeing all beings in the Self. Isha Upanishad 6: "And he who beholds all beings in the Self, and the Self in all beings, he never turns away from it."

And Isha 7: "When to a man who understands, the Self has become all things, what sorrow, what trouble can there be to him who once beheld that unity?"

This is related to resentment and not identical to it. The verses are about unity and the end of sorrow, and do not describe a grudge. Reading them as speaking to one is ours.
