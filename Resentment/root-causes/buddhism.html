Resentment

Holding on to an old hurt, so the past keeps paying rent in your head.

Root causes

The Dhammapada starts from the mind. Dhp 1: "Intention is the leader of things; intention is first, they're made by intention. If with corrupt intent you speak or act, suffering follows you, like a wheel, the ox's foot."

Dhp 3 shows the habit that keeps a grudge: the story told again and again. "'They abused me, they hit me! They beat me, they robbed me!' For those who bear such a grudge, hatred is never laid to rest."

This is related to resentment and not identical to it. Dhp 1 is about intention in general, and Dhp 3 about the repeated tale. Reading the repeated tale as the root is ours.
