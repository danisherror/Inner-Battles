The Bible shows resentment growing from a sense of being passed over, and from bitterness left to grow.

- **Favour.** Genesis 37:4: "when his brothers saw that their father loved him more than all his brothers, they hated him and could not speak peaceably to him."
- **A root.** Hebrews 12:15: "See to it that no one fails to obtain the grace of God, that no root of bitterness springs up and causes trouble and through it many become defiled."
- **Anger left overnight.** Ephesians 4:26-27: "Be angry but do not sin; do not let the sun go down on your anger, and do not make room for the devil."

This is related to resentment and not identical to it. Genesis 37 is about hatred between brothers, and the others are about bitterness and anger in general. Reading them as roots of a grudge is ours, and Joseph's brothers were the ones who wronged him.
