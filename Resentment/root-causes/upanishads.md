The Katha Upanishad names seeing difference as what keeps a person circling. Katha 2.1.10: "What is here (visible in the world), the same is there (invisible in Brahman); and what is there, the same is here. He who sees any difference here (between Brahman and the world), goes from death to death."

This is related to resentment and not identical to it. The verse is about the difference between Brahman and the world, and not about difference between people. Reading it as a root of a grudge, which sets "me" against "the one who wronged me", is ours.
