A story

Gideon, "the least in my family"

In the Book of Judges, the Lord turns to Gideon and says: "Go in this might of yours and deliver Israel from the hand of Midian; I hereby commission you" (6:14).

Gideon answers: "But sir, how can I deliver Israel? My clan is the weakest in Manasseh, and I am the least in my family" (6:15).

The reply does not argue with him about his clan or his place. It says: "But I will be with you, and you shall strike down the Midianites, every one of them" (6:16).

What it shows: Gideon's doubt is stated plainly, and the answer moves the question from what he is to who is with him. The passage is about a calling and not about impostor feelings; it is read here because it shows doubt named openly and answered.
