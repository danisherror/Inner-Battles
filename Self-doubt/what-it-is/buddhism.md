Buddhist texts have no word for self-doubt as we use it. **Doubt** (*vicikiccha*) is the fifth of the five hindrances, but it is doubt about the teaching and the path, so it is related to self-doubt and not identical to it (see [sloth in Buddhism](../../Sloth/what-it-is/buddhism.html) for the list).

SN 46.51 describes how doubt feeds: "And what fuels the arising of doubt, or, when it has arisen, makes it increase and grow? There are things that are grounds for doubt."

The Dhammapada has a chapter on the self that speaks to the other side. Dhp 157: "If you knew your self as beloved, you'd look after it so well." Dhp 160: "One is indeed the lord of oneself, for who else would be one's lord?"
