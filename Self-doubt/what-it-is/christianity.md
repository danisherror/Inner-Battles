The Bible has no word for impostor syndrome, but it records several people who doubt that they are the right person for the task.

- **Moses**: "Who am I that I should go to Pharaoh and bring the Israelites out of Egypt?" (Exodus 3:11). Later: "I have never been eloquent... I am slow of speech and slow of tongue" (4:10).
- **Gideon**: "how can I deliver Israel? My clan is the weakest in Manasseh, and I am the least in my family" (Judges 6:15).
- **Jeremiah**: "Truly I do not know how to speak, for I am only a boy" (Jeremiah 1:6).

In each case the answer is the same: "I will be with you" (Exodus 3:12; Judges 6:16; see also Jeremiah 1:8).

This is related to self-doubt and not identical to it. These people doubt their fitness for a calling, and the texts do not describe a general feeling of being a fraud.
