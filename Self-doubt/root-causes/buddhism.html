Self-doubt

Doubting your ability or worth, and feeling like a fraud despite what you have done.

Root causes

The Buddhist texts do not trace self-doubt itself, but SN 46.51 says how doubt in general is fed.

  • Grounds for doubt. "And what fuels the arising of doubt, or, when it has arisen, makes it increase and grow? There are things that are grounds for doubt." Doubt grows on what we give it to grow on.
  • Not knowing the self. Dhp 157: "If you knew your self as beloved, you'd look after it so well." The verse implies that failing to care for the self comes from not knowing it as dear, and that is a reading.

This is related to self-doubt and not identical to it. The hindrance of doubt is mainly about the teaching and the path, and the roots here are our reading.
