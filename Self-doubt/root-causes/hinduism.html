Self-doubt

Root causes

The Gita traces Arjuna's collapse to confusion about duty and to the mind turned against its owner.

  • Confusion and anxiety. Arjuna's own account in 2.7: "I am confused about my duty, and am besieged with anxiety and faintheartedness."
  • The mind as enemy. 6.6: "For those who have conquered the mind, it is their friend. For those who have failed to do so, the mind works like an enemy." A mind that argues against us is the root in view.
  • Doubt without faith or knowledge. 4.40 says that "persons who possess neither faith nor knowledge, and who are of a doubting nature, suffer a downfall". The doubt is about the teaching, and the verse is not about self-doubt.

This is related to self-doubt and not identical to it. The verses speak of duty, the mind and faith, and the roots listed are our reading.
