The Bible places the root in looking at oneself alone, and says what the doubters of the Bible were told to look at instead.

- **Comparing self with task.** Moses asks "Who am I?" (Exodus 3:11), Gideon says "I am the least in my family" (Judges 6:15), and Jeremiah says "I am only a boy" (Jeremiah 1:6). Each measures themselves against the task and finds themselves short.
- **Where adequacy comes from.** Paul writes: "Not that we are qualified of ourselves to claim anything as coming from us; our qualification is from God" (2 Corinthians 3:5). The text is saying that the standard of being qualified of ourselves is not the right one.
- **Grace.** "By the grace of God I am what I am, and his grace toward me has not been in vain" (1 Corinthians 15:10).

This is related to self-doubt and not identical to it. The passages are about calling and grace, and reading them for the roots of self-doubt is ours.
