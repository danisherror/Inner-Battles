Root causes

The Bible's root is that we judge ourselves by our own eyes. Proverbs 16:2: "All one's ways may be pure in one's own eyes, but the Lord weighs the spirit."

Galatians 6:3: "For if those who are nothing think they are something, they deceive themselves."

This is related to denial and not identical to it. These verses are about self-assessment and about thinking too well of oneself, and do not speak of refusing to face a fact. Taking them as roots of self-deception is ours.
