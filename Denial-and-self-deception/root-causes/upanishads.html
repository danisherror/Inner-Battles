Denial and self-deception

Not letting yourself see the truth, and explaining away the discomfort that follows.

Upanishads lens

Root causes

The Upanishads name conceit and the lure of wealth. Mundaka 1.2.8: "Fools dwelling in darkness, wise in their own conceit, and puffed up with vain knowledge, go round and round staggering to and fro, like blind men led by the blind."

Katha 1.2.6 gives the cause of the child's blindness: "deluded by the delusion of wealth."

This is related to denial and not identical to it. The verses are about conceit and wealth, and do not speak of avoiding an uncomfortable fact. Taking them as roots of self-deception is ours. Mundaka 1.2.8 repeats the words of Katha 1.2.5, which are used in Ego and validation.
