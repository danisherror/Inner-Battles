Seneca says that some people turn their faults into virtues. *Letters* 28: "Some boast of their faults. Do you think that the man has any thought of mending his ways who counts over his vices as if they were virtues?"

Socrates describes how the politician was "wiser still by himself" than others thought him (Plato, *Apology*).

This is related to denial and not identical to it. Seneca is writing about boasting of faults and Plato about false wisdom. Reading them as roots of self-deception is ours.
