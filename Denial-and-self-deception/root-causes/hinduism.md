The Gita's root is ignorance that covers knowledge. Gita 5.15: "The living entities are deluded because their inner knowledge is covered by ignorance."

And 7.13: "Deluded by the three modes of Maya, people in this world are unable to know Me, the imperishable and eternal."

This is related to denial and not identical to it. These verses are about ignorance and illusion in general, and do not mention looking away from a fact. Reading them as roots of self-deception is ours.
