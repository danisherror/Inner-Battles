The Upanishads say what the deluded face. Katha 1.2.6: "'This is the world,' he thinks, 'there is no other;' — thus he falls again and again under my sway."

Mundaka 1.2.8 describes the going round: "go round and round staggering to and fro, like blind men led by the blind."

This is related to denial and not identical to it. The verses are about the Hereafter and about conceit, and do not mention a refusal to face a fact. Reading them as the cost of self-deception is ours.
