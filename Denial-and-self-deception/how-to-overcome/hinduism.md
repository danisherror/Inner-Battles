The Gita's counsel is to seek knowledge, and to use the mind as a friend and not an enemy.

1. **Ask a teacher.** Gita 4.34: "Learn the Truth by approaching a spiritual master. Inquire from him with reverence and render service unto him."
2. **Value knowledge.** Gita 4.38: "In this world, there is nothing as purifying as divine knowledge."
3. **Make the mind a friend.** Gita 6.5: "Elevate yourself through the power of your mind, and not degrade yourself, for the mind can be the friend and also the enemy of the self."
4. **Check the match between act and thought.** Gita 3.6 warns of those who restrain action "while continuing to dwell on sense objects in the mind", and "certainly delude themselves".

This is related to denial and not identical to it. These verses are about knowledge, a teacher and the mind, and the Gita sets them within its own path. Reading them as a way through self-deception is ours, and nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist.
