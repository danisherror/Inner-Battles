The Upanishads name the same pull as the choice of the pleasant over the good. Katha 1.2.1: "The good is one thing, the pleasant another; these two, having different objects, chain a man. It is well with him who clings to the good; he who chooses the pleasant, misses his end."

And 1.3.5: "He who has no understanding and whose mind (the reins) is never firmly held, his senses (horses) are unmanageable, like vicious horses of a charioteer."

This is related to addiction and not identical to it. The Katha describes a choice made by a person with understanding or without it, and does not describe a condition that takes the choice away. Reading it as an account of compulsion is ours. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and a doctor can help with stopping safely.
