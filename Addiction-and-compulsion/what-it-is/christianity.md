Paul describes what it is like to act against your own wish. Romans 7:15: "I do not understand my own actions. For I do not do what I want, but I do the very thing I hate."

And 7:19: "For I do not do the good I want, but the evil I do not want is what I do."

This is related to addiction and not identical to it. Paul is writing about sin and the law, and the verses are not about substances or behaviours. Reading them as an account of compulsion is ours. The tradition's word for what he describes is not applied to people who struggle with addiction here, and the site does not treat addiction as a sin. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and a doctor can help with stopping safely.
