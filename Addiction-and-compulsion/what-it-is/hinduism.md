Hinduism has no single word in the Bhagavad Gita for addiction, but it describes the pull. Gita 2.60: "The senses are so strong and turbulent, O son of Kunti, that they can forcibly carry away the mind even of a person endowed with discrimination who practices self-control."

And 3.34: "The senses naturally experience attachment and aversion to the sense objects, but do not be controlled by them, for they are way-layers and foes."

This is related to addiction and not identical to it. The verses are about the senses and the mind in general, and do not describe a medical condition. Reading them as an account of compulsion is ours. They say that even a disciplined person can be carried away, which is a reason to see the pull as common and not shameful. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and a doctor can help with stopping safely.
