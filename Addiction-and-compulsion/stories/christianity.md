**The one who seeks another drink**

Proverbs 23 gives a picture and not a tale: "Who has woe? Who has sorrow? Who has strife? Who has complaining? Who has wounds without cause? Who has redness of eyes? Those who linger late over wine".

The last verse gives the voice: "'They struck me,' you will say, 'but I was not hurt; they beat me, but I did not feel it. When shall I awake? I will seek another drink.'"

Paul's cry in Romans 7 answers it from another side: "Wretched person that I am! Who will rescue me from this body of death? Thanks be to God through Jesus Christ our Lord!"

**What it shows:** The proverb shows how the pull sounds from inside, and the second text shows a cry for help that is answered with thanks. They are two texts about different things, and linking them to addiction is ours. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and a doctor can help with stopping safely.
