The research read here describes costs mostly for alcohol and drugs. Kelly and colleagues write that "Alcohol and other drug (AOD) problems confer a global, prodigious burden of disease, disability, and premature mortality." The Cochrane review adds that alcohol use disorder "confers a prodigious burden of disease, disability, premature mortality, and high economic costs from lost productivity, accidents, violence, incarceration, and increased healthcare utilization." Leshner points to "the health and social costs associated with drug abuse and addiction".

These abstracts are about alcohol and other drugs, and do not cover phones, gambling or pornography.

Common effects, as people describe them and not findings from these papers: secrecy, money trouble, strained relationships, poor sleep, and a growing distance from what you used to enjoy. Shame often follows, and it tends to feed the pattern; see [shame and guilt](../../Shame-and-guilt/what-it-is/modern.html).

Struggling to stop is common, and it is nothing to be ashamed of. If you are finding it hard, please talk to a doctor, therapist or a local support service. Stopping some substances suddenly, including alcohol and certain medicines, can be dangerous, so ask a doctor before stopping on your own (a standard safety point, not drawn from the papers below). If you are in danger, or thinking of harming yourself, contact your local emergency services now; [the International Association for Suicide Prevention points to a directory of verified helplines in over 150 countries](https://www.iasp.info/crisis-centres-helplines/).
