Proverbs describes the effects of drinking without softening them: "Who has woe? Who has sorrow? Who has strife? Who has complaining? Who has wounds without cause? Who has redness of eyes? Those who linger late over wine".

It ends with the voice of the one who cannot stop: "'They struck me,' you will say, 'but I was not hurt; they beat me, but I did not feel it. When shall I awake? I will seek another drink.'"

Romans 7:24 gives the cry: "Wretched person that I am! Who will rescue me from this body of death?"

This is related to addiction and not identical to it. Proverbs is wisdom writing about wine, and Romans 7:24 is about sin. Reading them together as effects of addiction is ours. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and a doctor can help with stopping safely.
