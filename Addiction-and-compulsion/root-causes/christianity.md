Paul describes a split between wanting and doing. Romans 7:18-19: "For I know that the good does not dwell within me, that is, in my flesh. For the desire to do the good lies close at hand, but not the ability."

And 7:23: "but I see in my members another law at war with the law of my mind, making me captive to the law of sin that dwells in my members."

In 1 Corinthians 6:12: "'All things are permitted for me,' but I will not be dominated by anything."

This is related to addiction and not identical to it. The passages are about sin and the flesh, and the cause they name is a divided self. Reading them as roots of compulsion is ours, and the site does not treat addiction as a sin. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and a doctor can help with stopping safely.
