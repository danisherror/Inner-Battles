The Buddhist texts name craving as the origin. SN 56.11: "It's the craving that leads to future lives, mixed up with relishing and greed, taking pleasure there wherever it alights. That is, craving for sensual pleasures, craving for continued existence, and craving for nonexistence."

Dhp 334 adds heedlessness: "When a man lives heedlessly, craving grows in them like a camel's foot creeper."

This is related to addiction and not identical to it. Craving here is the root of suffering for everyone, and the texts do not discuss addiction as a condition. Reading them as a root of compulsion is ours, and it should not be read as saying that people with an addiction have caused it by wanting. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and a doctor can help with stopping safely.
