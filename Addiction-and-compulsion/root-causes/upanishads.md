The Katha puts the cause in who is holding the reins. 1.3.3-4: "Know the Self to be sitting in the chariot, the body to be the chariot, the intellect (buddhi) the charioteer, and the mind the reins. The senses they call the horses, the objects of the senses their roads."

1.3.5: "He who has no understanding and whose mind (the reins) is never firmly held, his senses (horses) are unmanageable, like vicious horses of a charioteer."

This is related to addiction and not identical to it. The image is of the person as a whole and is not an account of the causes of a condition. Reading it as a root of compulsion is ours, and it should not be used to say that people who struggle lack understanding. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and a doctor can help with stopping safely.
