Root causes

The Gita describes a chain that starts with dwelling on the object. 2.62: "While contemplating on the objects of the senses, one develops attachment to them. Attachment leads to desire, and from desire arises anger."

2.63 continues: "Anger leads to clouding of judgment, which results in bewilderment of memory. When memory is bewildered, the intellect gets destroyed; and when the intellect is destroyed, one is ruined."

This is related to addiction and not identical to it. The chain is a general account of how desire grows and ends in anger and confusion, and it does not mention substances or a medical cause. Reading it as a root of compulsion is ours. The word "ruined" is the translator's, and it should not be taken as a verdict on a person. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and a doctor can help with stopping safely.
