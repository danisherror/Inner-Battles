The Gita counsels moderation, practice and not being ruled by the senses.

1. **Do not be controlled.** 3.34: "The senses naturally experience attachment and aversion to the sense objects, but do not be controlled by them".
2. **Keep a steady rhythm.** 6.17: "those who are temperate in eating and recreation, balanced in work, and regulated in sleep, can mitigate all sorrows by practicing Yog."
3. **Practise, and let go.** 6.35: "the mind is indeed very difficult to restrain. But by practice and detachment, it can be controlled."

It is also honest about relapse. 2.59: "Aspirants may restrain the senses from their objects of enjoyment, but the taste for the sense objects remains."

This is related to addiction and not identical to it. These verses are about the disciplined life and not about treatment. Reading them as a way through addiction is ours, and nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and a doctor can help with stopping safely.
