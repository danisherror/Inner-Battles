How to overcome it

The Buddhist texts counsel digging up the root, and trying again.

  1. Go to the root. Dhp 337: "dig up the root of craving, as you'd dig up grass in search of roots."
  2. Let it go. SN 56.11: "the fading away and cessation of that very same craving no residue left behind; giving it away, letting it go, releasing it, and not clinging to it."
  3. Do not be broken by a slip. Dhp 337: "Don't let Māra break you again and again, like a stream breaking a reed."
  4. Know it can fall away. Dhp 336: "their sorrows fall from them, like a drop from a lotus-leaf."

This is related to addiction and not identical to it. The texts speak of craving in general and of a path of practice, and do not describe treatment. Reading them as a way through addiction is ours, and nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and a doctor can help with stopping safely.
