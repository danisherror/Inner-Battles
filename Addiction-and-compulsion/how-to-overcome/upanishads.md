The Katha counsels telling the good from the pleasant, and taking the reins.

1. **Tell them apart.** 1.2.2: "The good and the pleasant approach man: the wise goes round about them and distinguishes them."
2. **Take the reins.** 1.3.6: "But he who has understanding and whose mind is always firmly held, his senses are under control, like good horses of a charioteer."

This is related to addiction and not identical to it. The passages are about wisdom and understanding, and the image of holding reins should not be read as "just control it". The site makes no claim about how to practise this, and nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and a doctor can help with stopping safely.
