Distraction

A mind pulled in many directions at once, unable to stay with what matters.

How to overcome it

The Gita points to bringing the mind back, steady attention, and one-pointed concentration.

  1. Bring it back, every time. Gita 6.26: "Whenever and wherever the restless and unsteady mind wanders, one should bring it back and continually focus it on God."
  2. Be like a lamp without wind. Gita 6.19: "Just as a lamp in a windless place does not flicker, so the disciplined mind of a yogi remains steady in meditation on the Supreme."
  3. Concentrate on one point. Gita 6.12-13: "the yogi should strive to purify the mind by focusing it in meditation with one pointed concentration, controlling all thoughts and activities."
  4. Withdraw when you need to. Gita 2.58: "One who is able to withdraw the senses from their objects, just as a tortoise withdraws its limbs into its shell, is established in divine wisdom."

This is related to distraction and not identical to it. The verses are about meditation in general, and nothing here replaces a doctor if you cannot focus even when you want to. Reading them as advice for a scattered day is ours.
