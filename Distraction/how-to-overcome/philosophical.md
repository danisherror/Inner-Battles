The Stoics' advice is to stay with a few things, and to stand firm in the noise.

1. **Linger over a few.** Seneca, *Letters* 2.2: "You must linger among a limited number of master-thinkers, and digest their works, if you would derive ideas which shall win firm hold in your mind."
2. **Own only what you can read.** Seneca, 2.3: "since you cannot read all the books which you may possess, it is enough to possess only as many books as you can read."
3. **Be the promontory.** Marcus Aurelius, *Meditations* 4: "Be like the promontory against which the waves continually break, but it stands firm and tames the fury of the water around it."

This is related to distraction and not identical to it. Seneca is speaking of reading, and Marcus of equanimity in trouble. Nothing here replaces a doctor if you cannot focus even when you want to, and applying them to a scattered day is ours.
