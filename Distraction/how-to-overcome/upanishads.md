The Upanishads point to settling, to holding the senses back, and to leaving off other words.

1. **Be like pure water in pure water.** Katha 2.1.15: "As pure water poured into pure water remains the same, thus, O Gautama, is the Self of a thinker who knows."
2. **Hold the senses back.** Katha 2.3.10-11: "When the five instruments of knowledge stand still together with the mind, and when the intellect does not move, that is called the highest state. This, the firm holding back of the senses, is what is called Yoga. He must be free from thoughtlessness then, for Yoga comes and goes."
3. **Leave off other words.** Mundaka 2.2.5: "Know him alone as the Self, and leave off other words!"

This is related to distraction and not identical to it. The verses are about the Self and yoga, and nothing here replaces a doctor if you cannot focus even when you want to. Reading them as advice for a scattered day is ours.
