Root causes

SN 46.51 names what feeds a restless, scattered mind. "And what fuels the arising of restlessness and remorse, or, when they have arisen, makes them increase and grow? There is the unsettled mind. Frequent irrational application of mind to that fuels the arising of restlessness and remorse, or, when they have arisen, makes them increase and grow."

This is related to distraction and not identical to it. The sutta is about restlessness and remorse as a hindrance in meditation, and is not about everyday distraction. Reading "the unsettled mind" as a root of scattered attention is ours.

Sources

  • SN 46.51 (external link)

The quotation was read in Bhikkhu Sujato's translation via the SuttaCentral bilarasuttas API on 2026-11-14. It joins consecutive lines of the sutta (4.1 to 4.4). SN 46.51 is also used in Self-doubt for another hindrance, and the Pali term for restlessness was not examined.
