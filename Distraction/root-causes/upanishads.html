Distraction

A mind pulled in many directions at once, unable to stay with what matters.

Upanishads lens

Root causes

The Katha Upanishad pictures the mind dispersing among differences. Katha 2.1.14: "As rain-water that has fallen on a mountain-ridge runs down the rocks on all sides, thus does he, who sees a difference between qualities, run after them on all sides."

This is related to distraction and not identical to it. The verse is about seeing difference between qualities, and not about attention as such. Reading "run after them on all sides" as a picture of scattered attention is ours.
