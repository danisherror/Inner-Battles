Distraction

A mind pulled in many directions at once, unable to stay with what matters.

Root causes

The Bible touches two roots. One is a restless appetite for new things. Ecclesiastes 1:8: "All things are wearisome, more than one can express; the eye is not satisfied with seeing or the ear filled with hearing."

The other is being taken up with many tasks. Luke 10:40: "Martha was distracted by her many tasks".

This is related to distraction and not identical to it. Ecclesiastes is a reflection on how the world never finishes, and Luke 10 is about a hostess. Reading them as roots of distraction is ours.
