Upanishads lens

Effects on life

The Katha Upanishad says what following outward things leads to. Katha 2.1.2: "Children follow after outward pleasures, and fall into the snare of wide-spread death. Wise men only, knowing the nature of what is immortal, do not look for anything stable here among things unstable."

This is related to distraction and not identical to it. The verse is about outward pleasures and death, and does not describe attention. Reading it as the toll of constant outward pulling is ours.
