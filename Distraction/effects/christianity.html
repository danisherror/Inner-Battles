Effects on life

The Bible shows what a divided mind costs.

  • Instability. James 1:8: "double-minded and unstable in every way".
  • Worry. Luke 10:41: "you are worried and distracted by many things".
  • Chasing wind. Ecclesiastes 4:6: "Better is a handful with quiet than two handfuls with toil and a chasing after wind."

This is related to distraction and not identical to it. These verses speak of doubt, worry and toil in general. Reading them as the toll of scattered attention is ours.
