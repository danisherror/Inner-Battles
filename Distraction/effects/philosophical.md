Seneca describes what scattering does to what we take in. *Letters* 2.2: "Everywhere means nowhere. When a person spends all his time in foreign travel, he ends by having many acquaintances, but no friends."

And 2.3: "a plant which is often moved can never grow strong. There is nothing so efficacious that it can be helpful while it is being shifted about. And in reading of many books is distraction."

This is related to distraction and not identical to it. Seneca is speaking of restless travel and reading, and not of scattered attention in a modern day. Reading him as describing its effects is ours.
