Effects on life

The Gita names what an uncontrolled mind lacks. Gita 2.66: "For one who never unites the mind with God there is no peace; and how can one who lacks peace be happy?"

It follows on from the earlier half of the verse: "an undisciplined person, who has not controlled the mind and senses, can neither have a resolute intellect nor steady contemplation on God."

This is related to distraction and not identical to it. The verse is about a mind not united with God and the lack of peace that follows, and it is not a description of everyday distraction. Reading it as the toll of scattered attention is ours.
