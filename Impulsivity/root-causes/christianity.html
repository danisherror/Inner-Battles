Inner battle

Impulsivity

Acting on the urge of the moment and giving up a larger, later good for a smaller, sooner one.

Root causes

The Bible does not give a theory of impulse, but Paul describes the split. Romans 7:15: "I do not understand my own actions. For I do not do what I want, but I do the very thing I hate."

Esau's reply in Genesis 25 shows the pull of the present need: "I am about to die; of what use is a birthright to me?" (25:32).

This is related to impulsivity and not identical to it. Romans 7 is about sin and the divided will, and Genesis 25 is about hunger. Reading them as roots of acting on impulse is ours.
