Inner battle

Impulsivity

Acting on the urge of the moment and giving up a larger, later good for a smaller, sooner one.

Effects on life

The Bible shows what haste costs.

  • Want. Proverbs 21:5: "The plans of the diligent lead surely to abundance, but everyone who is hasty comes only to want."
  • Hopelessness of the rash speaker. Proverbs 29:20: "Do you see someone who is hasty in speech? There is more hope for a fool than for anyone like that."
  • A birthright lost. Hebrews 12:16 says of Esau: "who sold his birthright for a single meal".

This is related to impulsivity and not identical to it. The proverbs set haste against diligence, and Hebrews 12:16 uses Esau as a warning. Reading them as the toll of acting on impulse is ours, and these verses should not be used to shame anyone.
