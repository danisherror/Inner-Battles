The Bible speaks of self-control and its lack in short, blunt sayings. Proverbs 25:28: "Like a city breached, without walls, is one who lacks self-control."

Proverbs 14:29: "Whoever is slow to anger has great understanding, but one who has a hasty temper exalts folly." And Proverbs 29:20: "Do you see someone who is hasty in speech? There is more hope for a fool than for anyone like that."

This is related to impulsivity and not identical to it. The proverbs speak of haste in temper and speech, and of lacking self-control, and do not describe impulsivity as a pattern. Reading them as speaking to it is ours.
