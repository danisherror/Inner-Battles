Inner battle

Impulsivity

Acting on the urge of the moment and giving up a larger, later good for a smaller, sooner one.

How to overcome it

The Bible's counsel is to slow down, to train, and to receive self-control as a gift.

  1. Slow down. James 1:19: "let everyone be quick to listen, slow to speak, slow to anger".
  2. Seek understanding. Proverbs 14:29: "Whoever is slow to anger has great understanding".
  3. Train. 1 Corinthians 9:25: "Athletes exercise self-control in all things; they do it to receive a perishable wreath, but we an imperishable one."
  4. Let it grow. Galatians 5:22-23: "the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, generosity, faithfulness, gentleness, and self-control."

This is related to impulsivity and not identical to it. These verses are about patience and self-control in general, and nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist for impulsive behaviour that is causing harm.
