The Upanishads see greed as a search for fulfilment in the wrong place.

- **Pleasant versus good.** Katha 1.2.1-2 distinguishes *preyas* (the pleasant) from *shreyas* (the good). The less wise choose the pleasant for the sake of getting and keeping.
- **Fear from a second.** Brihadaranyaka 1.4.2-3 says the Self felt fear, and then no delight, because it was alone. It desired a second. The sense of lack, and so the urge to acquire, comes from separateness.
- **Not knowing what is enough.** Katha 1.1.27 says no one is satisfied by wealth, and 1.1.26 has Nachiketa refuse offered riches and long life because they wear out the senses.
- **Looking outward.** Katha 2.1.1 says the self-existent pierced the openings of the senses outward, so people look at the outside and not the inner Self.
