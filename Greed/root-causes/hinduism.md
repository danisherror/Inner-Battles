Hindu thought traces greed to **desire fed by ignorance**.

- **Desire grows by feeding.** Gita 3.37-39 says desire born of *rajas* is insatiable, "like fire", and covers knowledge. Manusmriti 2.94 is often quoted for the same idea: desire is never quenched by indulging it, as fire is not put out by ghee.
- **Attachment to objects.** Gita 2.62-63 describes the chain: dwelling on objects produces attachment, then desire, anger, delusion and loss of reason.
- **The sense of "mine".** *Mamata* (possessiveness) rides on *ahamkara*. Gita 16.13-14 gives the thought, "this wealth is mine, and more will be mine."
- **Rajas.** Gita 14.17 says greed (*lobha*) arises from the quality of *rajas*, along with restless activity.
