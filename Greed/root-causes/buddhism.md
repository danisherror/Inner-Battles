Buddhism's answer is **craving rooted in ignorance**.

- **Craving.** The first sermon (SN 56.11) says suffering arises from craving: for sense pleasures, for becoming and for non-becoming. Greed is craving aimed at possessions.
- **Ignorance of impermanence and not-self.** We grasp because we believe things can make a lasting "me" secure. Seeing impermanence loosens the grip, as in the teaching on the five aggregates (SN 22.59).
- **Dependent origination.** In the standard chain, contact leads to feeling, feeling to craving, craving to clinging (SN 12.2). Greed grows at the step from pleasant feeling to craving.
- **Unwise attention.** Clinging builds on repeatedly dwelling on what is attractive. The suttas call attending to the attractive sign a feeder of sensual desire (SN 46.51).
