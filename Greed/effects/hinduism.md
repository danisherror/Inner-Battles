The Gita describes where greed leads.

- **Restlessness and fear.** Gita 16.13-16 shows the greedy mind caught in "many thoughts", ending in anxiety and attachment to enjoyment, and falling "into a foul hell."
- **Self-destruction.** Gita 16.21 names desire, anger and greed as three gates to the ruin of the self.
- **Unending hunger.** Gita 3.39 calls desire an insatiable fire.
- **Loss of the sharing that holds society together.** Gita 3.12 says one who enjoys what the gods have given, without giving in return, is a thief. The *Mahabharata* shows the social side in Duryodhana, whose refusal to share land contributes to a war that destroys his family.
- **No peace.** Gita 2.70-71 compares a mind that is full without being agitated to the ocean, and says peace does not come to the one who craves.
