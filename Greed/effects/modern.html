Deadly sin

Greed

Wanting more than you need, and never feeling you have enough.

Effects on life

Greed tends to cost the things money can't buy back.

  • Well-being. People who put wealth and image first report more anxiety and less satisfaction on average.
  • Relationships. Treating people as means to an end, or fighting over money, wears trust down.
  • Ethics. Some studies link a strong focus on self-interest and wealth with more rule-breaking, although this research is debated and not every result has replicated.
  • Time and health. Chasing more means long hours, stress and less rest.
  • The treadmill. Because satisfaction fades, the gains rarely feel like enough. Society pays too when greed drives fraud, exploitation or harm to the environment.
