Upanishads lens

Effects on life

In the Upanishads the cost of greed is staying on the wrong path.

  • Death in the end. Katha 1.1.26-27 has Nachiketa tell Death that wealth lasts only till tomorrow and wears away the vigour of the senses.
  • Choosing the pleasant. Katha 1.2.2 says that the one who picks the pleasant (preyas) loses the goal.
  • Wealth cannot save. Brihadaranyaka 2.4.3 (repeated at 4.5.3) reports the point as Yajnavalkya states it: "there is no hope of immortality by wealth" (Müller translation).
  • Small things, small joy. Chandogya 7.23-24 says there is no happiness in the small; the one who sees only the finite lives in the finite.
