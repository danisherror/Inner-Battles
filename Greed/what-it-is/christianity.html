Greed

Wanting more than you need, and never feeling you have enough.

Christianity lens

What it is

Christian tradition calls it avarice or covetousness (Latin avaritia): an excessive desire for money and possessions. The tenth commandment forbids coveting a neighbour's goods (Exodus 20:17), and the Catechism treats avarice as a sin against it (CCC 2534-2536).

The New Testament is direct:

  • "The love of money is a root of all kinds of evil" (1 Timothy 6:10). The target is the love of money, not money itself.
  • "You cannot serve God and wealth" (Matthew 6:24).
  • "Be on your guard against all kinds of greed; for one's life does not consist in the abundance of possessions" (Luke 12:15).
  • Greed is called idolatry in Colossians 3:5.

Aquinas places avarice among the capital sins (Summa II-II q.118) as an immoderate love of possessing.
