Greed is wanting more than you need, and more than is good for you or fair to others, while never feeling you have enough. It is not the same as ambition or enjoying nice things. The test is whether wanting has turned into a compulsion that pushes out honesty, generosity and contentment.

Signs include:

- Satisfaction that fades as soon as a goal is reached ("hedonic adaptation"), so the target keeps moving.
- Hoarding, or being unable to give when you could easily afford to.
- Cutting corners, or taking from others, to get more.
- Measuring your worth by what you own.

Researchers find that people who rank wealth and image above relationships and growth report lower well-being, on average.
