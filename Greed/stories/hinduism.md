**Duryodhana and the five villages**

In the *Mahabharata*, the Pandavas lose their kingdom through a rigged dice game and spend years in exile. When they return, the kingdom is not given back.

Before war, Krishna goes to the Kaurava court as peace envoy (Udyoga Parva). He lowers the Pandavas' demand again and again, down to just five villages, enough for each brother to live in. Elders, including Bhishma, Drona and Vidura, urge Duryodhana to accept.

Duryodhana refuses. He says he will not give up even as much land as the point of a needle can pierce. The war follows, and by its end Duryodhana, his brothers and nearly his whole army are dead.

**What it shows:** Greed turns a small and fair share into a loss. Duryodhana could have lived in peace with most of what he had, but "mine" had no limit, and he lost it all.
