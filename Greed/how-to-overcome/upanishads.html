Deadly sin

Greed

Wanting more than you need, and never feeling you have enough.

How to overcome it

The Upanishadic remedy is to look for fullness within.

  • Choose shreyas. Katha 1.2.1-2: the wise pick the good over the pleasant. Nachiketa's refusal of wealth in 1.1.26-29 is the model.
  • Renounce to enjoy. Isha 1: "When thou hast surrendered all this, then thou mayest enjoy. Do not covet the wealth of any man!" (Müller translation).
  • Seek the Infinite. Chandogya 7.23 says only the infinite brings happiness. The practice is to turn attention to the Self (atman), through study, reflection and meditation.
  • Be given to giving. Taittiriya 1.11 (the teacher's farewell advice) tells the student to give with faith, in plenty, with modesty and with sympathy.
  • Find the Self in all. Isha 6-7: for one who sees the Self in all beings and all beings in the Self, "what sorrow, what trouble can there be" (Müller translation), so there is nothing to hoard against.
