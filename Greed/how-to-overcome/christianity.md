The Christian response is generosity, trust and contentment.

- **Give.** Acts 20:35 quotes Jesus: "It is more blessed to give than to receive." 2 Corinthians 9:7 says God loves a cheerful giver. Proverbs 11:24-25 says the one who gives freely grows richer.
- **Be content.** 1 Timothy 6:6-8: godliness with contentment is great gain; if we have food and clothing, we will be content. Hebrews 13:5 says to keep your life free from the love of money.
- **Trust.** Matthew 6:19-21 says to store treasures in heaven, "for where your treasure is, there your heart will be also."
- **Share.** Acts 2:44-45 describes believers sharing goods as any had need.
- **Tithing and almsgiving.** Traditional disciplines that put giving first.
- **Repentance and restitution.** Zacchaeus (Luke 19:8) gives half his goods to the poor and repays fourfold.
