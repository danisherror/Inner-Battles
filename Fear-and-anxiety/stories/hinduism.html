A story

Arjuna sees the universal form

In Chapter 11 of the Gita, Arjuna asks to see Krishna's universal form, and Krishna grants it. Arjuna is overwhelmed: "Having seen Your universal form that I had never seen before, I feel great joy. And yet, my mind trembles with fear. Please have mercy on me and again show me Your pleasing form" (11.45).

He asks to see Krishna in the form he knows: "I wish to see You in Your four-armed form, carrying the mace and disc, and wearing the crown" (11.46).

Krishna answers: "Be neither afraid nor bewildered on seeing this terrible form of Mine. Be free from fear and with a cheerful heart, behold Me once again in My personal form" (11.49). Sanjay then reports that Krishna "further consoled the frightened Arjun by assuming His gentle (two-armed) form" (11.50).

What it shows: Fear and joy arrive together in Arjuna, and the answer is reassurance and a form he can bear. The chapter is about the vision of the cosmic form and not about anxiety as such; the story is read here for how fear is met.
