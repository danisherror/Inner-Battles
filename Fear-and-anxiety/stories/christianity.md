**Peter on the water**

In Matthew 14 the disciples are in a boat "battered by the waves", with the wind against them. Jesus comes toward them walking on the sea. They are terrified and think he is a ghost, and he says: "Take heart, it is I; do not be afraid" (14:24-27).

Peter answers: "Lord, if it is you, command me to come to you on the water." Jesus says, "Come." Peter gets out of the boat and walks toward Jesus. "But when he noticed the strong wind, he became frightened, and, beginning to sink, he cried out, 'Lord, save me!'" (14:28-30).

"Jesus immediately reached out his hand and caught him, saying to him, 'You of little faith, why did you doubt?'" (14:31). When they got into the boat, the wind ceased.

**What it shows:** Peter walks while he is looking at Jesus, and sinks when he notices the wind, and in both cases the help is a hand held out at once. The story is about faith and not about anxiety as a condition. It does not blame him for being afraid; it shows fear arriving and a hand reaching out.
