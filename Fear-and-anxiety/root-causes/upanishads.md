The Katha Upanishad puts fear of death at the centre of the human situation, and speaks to it. Katha 1.2.18: "The knowing (Self) is not born, it dies not; it sprang from nothing, nothing sprang from it. The Ancient is unborn, eternal, everlasting; he is not killed, though the body is killed."

And 1.2.19: "If the killer thinks that he kills, if the killed thinks that he is killed, they do not understand; for this one does not kill, nor is that one killed."

This is related to fear and anxiety and not identical to them. The verses are about the Self and death, and say nothing of anxiety or of where it comes from. Reading them as naming the fear of death as a root of fear is ours.
