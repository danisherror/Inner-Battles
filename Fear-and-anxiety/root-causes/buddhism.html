Fear and anxiety

Root causes

MN 4 names causes of unskillful fear in those who go to the wilderness: faults in conduct, in livelihood, and a lack of understanding. Each time the wording is the same: they "summon unskillful fear and dread because of the defects of witlessness and stupidity", or of their conduct or livelihood.

The Dart (Snp 3.8) points to a deeper root: mortality. "Unforeseen and unknown is the extent of this mortal life— hard and short and bound to pain. There is no way that those born will not die."

Dhp 212 links fear to what we hold dear: "fear springs from what we hold dear".

This is related to fear and anxiety and not identical to them. MN 4 is about fear in the forest, Snp 3.8 about death, and Dhp 212 about attachment. Reading them together as roots of anxiety is ours.
