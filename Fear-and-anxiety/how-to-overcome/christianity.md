The Bible's answer to fear is presence and courage, not being told to feel nothing.

1. **Remember you are not alone.** Psalm 23:4: "Even though I walk through the darkest valley, I fear no evil, for you are with me; your rod and your staff, they comfort me."
2. **Be courageous with company.** Joshua 1:9: "Be strong and courageous; do not be frightened or dismayed, for the Lord your God is with you wherever you go."
3. **Remember the spirit you were given.** 2 Timothy 1:7: "for God did not give us a spirit of cowardice but rather a spirit of power and of love and of self-discipline."
4. **Let love push out fear.** 1 John 4:18: "There is no fear in love, but perfect love casts out fear".
5. **Take your cares somewhere.** Psalm 94:19: "When the cares of my heart are many, your consolations cheer my soul." (See also Philippians 4:6-7 in [Overthinking](../../Overthinking/how-to-overcome/christianity.html).)

This is related to fear and anxiety and not identical to them. These verses are about trust and courage in general, and nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist for intense or constant anxiety. They are not a command to stop feeling afraid.
