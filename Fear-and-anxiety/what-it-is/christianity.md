The Bible does not hide fear and anxiety. Psalm 56:3-4: "when I am afraid, I put my trust in you. In God, whose word I praise, in God I trust; I am not afraid; what can flesh do to me?"

Proverbs 12:25 names anxiety: "Anxiety weighs down the human heart, but a good word cheers it up." And Psalm 94:19: "When the cares of my heart are many, your consolations cheer my soul."

This is related to fear and anxiety as the modern lens describes them, and not identical to them. The texts are prayers and proverbs about trust and care, and do not describe anxiety as a condition or tell anyone to stop feeling it. An anxious person is not asked to hide it.
