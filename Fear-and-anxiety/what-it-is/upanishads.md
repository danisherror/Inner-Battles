The Upanishads do not name anxiety, and they speak of fear mostly by its absence. In the Katha Upanishad, the boy Nachiketas says of the world of heaven: "In the heaven-world there is no fear; thou art not there, O Death, and no one is afraid on account of old age" (1.1.12).

Katha 2.1.12 describes the person who knows the Self: "The person (purusha), of the size of a thumb, stands in the middle of the Self (body ?), as lord of the past and the future, and henceforward fears no more."

This is related to fear and anxiety and not identical to them. The passages are about death and knowledge of the Self, and do not describe anxiety as a condition. Reading them as speaking to it is ours.
