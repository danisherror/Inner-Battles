Philosophical lens

What it is

Aristotle gives a definition of fear. In the Rhetoric (2.5): "Fear may be defined as a pain or disturbance due to a mental picture of some destructive or painful evil in the future."

In the Nicomachean Ethics (3.6) he says people "even define fear as expectation of evil", and that not all fear is wrong: "to fear some things is even right and noble, and it is base not to fear them".

This fits the philosophical lens closely, since Aristotle's definition is of fear directly. It is a definition of fear in general and not of anxiety as a disorder, and it says nothing about what to do about it.
