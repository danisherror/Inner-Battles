Chandogya 7 builds a ladder of "better than", and at its end it puts the root of acting in a condition of the self. In 7.19-7.22 Sanatkumara says: "When one believes, then one perceives." "When one attends on a tutor (spiritual guide), then one believes." "When one performs all sacred duties, then one attends really on a tutor." And: "When one obtains bliss (in oneself), then one performs duties. One who does not obtain bliss, does not perform duties."

The chain runs from bliss back to the self's condition, and says that where it is lacking, nothing follows.

This is related to hopelessness and not identical to it. The passage is about the order in which knowledge arises, and says nothing of depression or despair. Reading it as a root of hopelessness is ours, and it should not be used to blame anyone who finds it hard to act. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist. If you are thinking of ending your life or harming yourself, please tell someone today and contact your local emergency services if you may act on it.
