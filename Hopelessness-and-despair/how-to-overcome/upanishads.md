The Upanishads answer with strength, steadiness and knowledge, and not with a technique. Mundaka 3.2.4: "Nor is that Self to be gained by one who is destitute of strength, or without earnestness, or without right meditation. But if a wise man strives after it by those means (by strength, earnestness, and right meditation), then his Self enters the home of Brahman."

Chandogya 7.14 holds that hope can be meditated on: "Meditate on hope."

This is related to hopelessness and not identical to it. The first passage is about gaining the Self and could be misread as blaming a person who lacks strength, and it is not that. Neither passage turns into steps, and the site makes no claim about how to practise them. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist. If you are thinking of ending your life or harming yourself, please tell someone today and contact your local emergency services if you may act on it.
