The Gita offers self-effort and patience, and it should be read with care.

1. **Rise.** 2.3: "Give up such petty weakness of heart and arise". This is said to a healthy warrior, and for someone who is ill it is not an instruction to try harder.
2. **Use the mind as an ally.** 6.5: "Elevate yourself through the power of your mind, and not degrade yourself, for the mind can be the friend and also the enemy of the self."
3. **Practise.** 6.35: "the mind is indeed very difficult to restrain. But by practice and detachment, it can be controlled."

This is related to hopelessness and not identical to it. These verses are about the disciplined life and do not speak of depression, and Krishna in 6.35 agrees that the mind is hard to restrain. Reading them as a way through hopelessness is ours, and they do not replace a doctor or therapist. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist. If you are thinking of ending your life or harming yourself, please tell someone today and contact your local emergency services if you may act on it.
