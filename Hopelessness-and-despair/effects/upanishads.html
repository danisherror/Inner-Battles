Hopelessness and despair

Believing that nothing will change, and that nothing you do will matter.

Effects on life

The Upanishads link hope and action. Chandogya 7.14: "Fired by hope does memory read the sacred hymns, perform sacrifices, desire sons and cattle, desire this world and the other." In 7.22: "When one obtains bliss (in oneself), then one performs duties. One who does not obtain bliss, does not perform duties."

The effect named is that without the inner condition, action does not follow.

This is related to hopelessness and not identical to it. Neither passage speaks of despair, and the second is about knowledge and not about mood. Reading them as effects of hopelessness is ours. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist. If you are thinking of ending your life or harming yourself, please tell someone today and contact your local emergency services if you may act on it.
