**Narada climbs the ladder**

In Chandogya 7 Narada asks Sanatkumara to teach him. Each time Sanatkumara names something and says to meditate on it, Narada asks: "Sir, is there something better than" it, and the answer is "Yes".

In 7.14: "Hope (āśā) is better than memory." Narada asks: "Sir, is there something better than hope?" "Yes, there is something better than hope." In 7.15: "Spirit (prâṇa) is better than hope."

**What it shows:** Hope is a step on a ladder and not its top, and Narada keeps asking. This is a moment in a dialogue and not a tale of despair, and linking it to hopelessness is ours.
