Christian practice answers gluttony with **temperance, thanksgiving and fasting**.

- **Temperance.** Titus 2:12 and Galatians 5:23 name self-control as a fruit of the Spirit.
- **Give thanks.** 1 Timothy 4:4-5 says everything created is good if received with thanksgiving. Grace before meals is a habit of gratitude.
- **Eat and drink for God's glory.** 1 Corinthians 10:31.
- **Fasting.** Jesus assumes fasting as a normal practice (Matthew 6:16-18). The Church keeps seasons such as Lent. Fasting is meant to teach freedom, not to punish.
- **Share the table.** Luke 14:12-14 asks that feasts include the poor.
- **Know your limit.** Proverbs 25:16: "If you have found honey, eat only enough for you."
