Gluttony

Over-indulgence, taking more than you need.

How to overcome it

Hindu practice answers it with moderation, sattvic food and fasting with purpose.

  • Yukta-ahara. Gita 6.17: moderate in eating and recreation, in action and in rest.
  • Sattvic food. Gita 17.8: foods that increase life, strength, health and cheer, and are tasty and nourishing.
  • Offer the food. Gita 3.13 says those who eat what is left after offering are freed from sin, and that those who cook only for themselves eat sin. Giving thanks and sharing before eating is a standard practice (prasada).
  • Fasting. Upavasa on set days (such as Ekadashi) trains self-control. It is traditionally done with a purpose, and the Gita (17.5-6) warns against severe, harmful austerity.
  • Sense control. Gita 2.58 compares the sage to a tortoise drawing in its limbs.
