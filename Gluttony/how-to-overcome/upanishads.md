The Upanishadic response is to treat food with respect and to train the "reins" of the mind.

- **Honour food.** Taittiriya 3.7-8: "Let him never abuse food... never shun food" (Müller translation). Eat with awareness and gratitude.
- **Share it.** Taittiriya 3.10 praises giving food: "If he gives food amply, food is given to him amply."
- **Pure food, pure mind.** Chandogya 7.26.2: "When the intellectual aliment has been purified, the whole nature becomes purified" (Müller translation). Traditional readings differ on whether *ahara* means literal food or all that the senses take in.
- **Drive the chariot.** Katha 1.3.3-9: the one who has a skilled driver (a steady intellect) and holds the reins (the mind) reaches the end of the road.
- **Choose *shreyas*.** Katha 1.2.1-2: choose the good over the merely pleasant.
