Gluttony

Over-indulgence, taking more than you need.

Effects on life

The Upanishads connect eating with the quality of mind and of life.

  • Mind follows food. Chandogya 6.5.4: the mind is made of food, and 7.26.2 adds, in Müller's rendering, that when the "aliment" is purified the whole nature is purified (commentators disagree on whether this means literal food). Misuse of food clouds the mind.
  • Wasted gift. Taittiriya 3.7-8 instructs never to abuse or shun food, which suggests that both overindulgence and waste are a failure of respect.
  • Wrong direction. Katha 1.2.2: the one who chooses the pleasant loses the aim.
  • Unruly senses. Katha 1.3.4-5: the senses of the unsteady person are "like the wicked horses of a charioteer."
