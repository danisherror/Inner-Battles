Hinduism has no single word equal to the Christian sin of gluttony. The closest ideas are **lobha** (greed), **kama** (craving) applied to taste, and the failure of **sense-control** (*indriya-nigraha*), with the tongue seen as the hardest sense to master.

The Gita speaks to it directly. In 6.16-17, yoga is "not for one who eats too much or fasts too much", and it belongs to the one who is moderate in eating, recreation, work and sleep. In 17.8-10, it describes the food that each *guna* prefers: *sattvic* food is nourishing and pleasant, while *rajasic* food (very bitter, sour, salty, hot or dry) and *tamasic* food (stale, tasteless, impure) belong to the other two.

This is related to gluttony and not identical to it: the focus is balance, not sin.
