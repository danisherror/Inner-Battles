What it is

Gluttony is over-indulgence: taking more than you need, or taking it in a way that is driven by craving instead of hunger. It is usually pictured as overeating, but it covers drink, and by extension any habit of consuming past the point of benefit, such as bingeing on screens or sweets.

Aquinas listed five forms: eating too soon, too expensively, too much, too eagerly and too daintily. Modern research on hedonic eating (eating for pleasure when not hungry) and emotional eating fits the same picture.

It is not the same as an eating disorder. Binge eating disorder and similar conditions are medical problems, and they call for care, not moral blame.
