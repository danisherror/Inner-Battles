Anger harms the angry person first.

- **Hatred feeds on itself.** Dhammapada 5: hatreds never cease through hatred.
- **Burnt by one's own fire.** The Buddha's image of "anger with a poisoned root and a honeyed tip" (SN 1.71) shows that anger can feel good in the moment and bring suffering after.
- **Ugly results.** AN 7.64 (Kodhana Sutta) lists seven things that please an enemy about an angry person, including that they become ugly, sleep badly and lose wealth and friends.
- **Karma.** Harsh speech and harmful action from anger lead to suffering for oneself and others.
- **Blocks meditation.** Ill will is one of the five hindrances to concentration.
