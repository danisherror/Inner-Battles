Effects on life

Scripture lists the damage anger does.

  • Foolishness. Proverbs 14:29: "Whoever is slow to anger has great understanding, but one who has a hasty temper exalts folly." Ecclesiastes 7:9 says anger lodges in the bosom of fools.
  • Strife. Proverbs 15:18: "Those who are hot-tempered stir up strife."
  • Murder in the heart. Matthew 5:21-22 and 1 John 3:15 link hatred with killing.
  • Giving the devil a foothold. Ephesians 4:26-27 warns that nursed anger gives the devil an opportunity.
  • Unrighteousness. James 1:20: human anger does not produce God's righteousness.
  • Cost to leaders. Moses, for striking the rock in anger, is not allowed to enter the promised land (Numbers 20:10-12).
