Effects on life

The Gita describes how anger undoes a person.

  • Ruin. Gita 2.63: from anger comes delusion, from delusion loss of memory, from loss of memory loss of reason, and from that destruction.
  • Gate to hell. Gita 16.21 calls desire, anger and greed the three gates to self-destruction.
  • Loss of peace. Gita 2.66 says that there is no peace for the unsteady mind.
  • Harm to others. In the Mahabharata, Duryodhana's hatred, and Draupadi's vow of revenge after her humiliation, run through the whole war. The epic treats both the wrong done and the anger it raised as feeding the catastrophe.
  • Freedom in the opposite. Gita 5.23 says the one who can resist the impulse of desire and anger before leaving the body is a yogi and is happy. 5.26 says those free of desire and anger find liberation.
