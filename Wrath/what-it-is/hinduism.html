Uncontrolled anger, resentment and the wish to get even.

What it is

The Sanskrit word is krodha, anger, one of the six inner enemies (shadripu). It is closely tied to desire (kama): anger arises when desire is blocked.

In the Gita, 3.37 names desire and anger, born of rajas, as the all-devouring enemy. In 16.21 they are two of the three gates to ruin. In 16.4, anger is listed among the traits of the asuri (demonic) nature. In contrast, the divine qualities of 16.1-3 include akrodha (freedom from anger), kshama (forgiveness), shanti (peace) and ahimsa (non-harm).

The Yoga Sutras (2.35) add that where ahimsa is firmly established, hostility ceases in its presence.
