How to overcome it

The Upanishadic path to freedom from anger is the training of the self and the vision of unity.

  • Da, da, da. Brihadaranyaka 5.2.3 reports the thunder's three teachings: damyata (control yourselves), datta (give), dayadhvam (be compassionate).
  • Calm, controlled, patient. Brihadaranyaka 4.4.23: shanto danta uparatas titikshuh samahito.
  • See the Self in all. Isha 6: one who sees all beings in the Self does not hate.
  • Hold the reins. Katha 1.3.3-9: let the intellect be the driver and the mind the reins, and guide the senses.
  • Ask for calm. Katha 1.1.10 shows a wish that a father's anger be calmed, a model of asking for peace.
  • Study and meditate. Later Vedanta teaches reflection on unity as a way to weaken the grounds of enmity.
