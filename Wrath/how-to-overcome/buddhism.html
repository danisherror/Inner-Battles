Deadly sin

Wrath

Uncontrolled anger, resentment and the wish to get even.

How to overcome it

Buddhism has detailed practice for anger.

  • Non-hatred. Dhammapada 5 and 223: conquer anger by non-anger, the bad by good, the miser by giving, the liar by truth.
  • Metta. Loving-kindness practice (Sn 1.8) wishes well for all beings, including those you resent. Start with yourself and a friend, and move toward the difficult person.
  • The saw simile. The Kakacupama Sutta (MN 21) says that even if bandits were sawing you apart limb by limb, anyone who gave way to hatred would not be following the teaching.
  • Five ways to remove resentment. AN 5.161: cultivate loving-kindness, compassion or equanimity toward the person, forget or ignore them, or reflect on karma as one's own.
  • Patience. Khanti is called the highest austerity (Dhammapada 184).
  • Notice and let go. Mindful attention to the feeling in the body without acting on it.
