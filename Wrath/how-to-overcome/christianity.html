How to overcome it

Christian practice answers anger with gentleness, forgiveness and love of enemies.

  • Be slow to anger. James 1:19: "quick to listen, slow to speak, slow to anger."
  • Answer softly. Proverbs 15:1: "A soft answer turns away wrath." Proverbs 16:32: "One who is slow to anger is better than the mighty."
  • Settle things the same day. Ephesians 4:26-27, and Matthew 5:23-24 (be reconciled before bringing your gift).
  • Forgive. Ephesians 4:32 and Colossians 3:13: forgive as you have been forgiven. In the Lord's Prayer: "forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven our debtors" (Matthew 6:12).
  • Do not repay evil. Romans 12:17-21: "Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good."
  • Love enemies and pray for them (Matthew 5:44).
  • Confession and prayer, and where needed counsel.
