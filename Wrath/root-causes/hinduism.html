Wrath

Uncontrolled anger, resentment and the wish to get even.

Root causes

Hindu thought traces anger to blocked desire and to the ego that feels wronged.

  • The chain. Gita 2.62-63: dwelling on objects leads to attachment, attachment to desire, and "from desire comes anger" (kamat krodho 'bhijayate). Anger leads to delusion, loss of memory, loss of reason and ruin.
  • Rajas. Gita 3.37 says that desire and anger are born of rajas.
  • Ego and "mine." Gita 16.18 lists ahamkara (ego), power, arrogance, desire and anger together, as the traits that lead people to hate the Divine in themselves and others.
  • Ignorance. Gita 16.4 ties anger to ajnana (ignorance).
  • Karma and fate. Gita 18.60 says that one is bound by action born of one's own nature, and so acts even against one's wish.
