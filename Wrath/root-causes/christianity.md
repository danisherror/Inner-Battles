Christian writers find anger's roots in **pride, wounded self-love and unforgiveness**.

- **From pride.** Gregory the Great (*Moralia* 31.45) lists anger as a child of pride. If I must be honoured, any slight enrages me.
- **Desire for vengeance.** Aquinas (*ST* II-II q.158) describes anger as a desire for retribution that goes beyond reason.
- **Inner life.** James 4:1-2 asks, "Those conflicts and disputes among you, where do they come from? Do they not come from your cravings that are at war within you?"
- **Fall.** Cain's anger follows the rejection of his offering (Genesis 4:5-7), and God warns him that "sin is lurking at the door."
- **Unforgiveness.** The parable of the unforgiving servant (Matthew 18:21-35) shows resentment as the refusal to give what one has received.
