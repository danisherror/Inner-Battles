Root causes

The Upanishads place the root in separateness and unruly senses and mind.

  • A second. Brihadaranyaka 1.4.2-3 says fear arose because there was a second. Where there is another, there can be conflict.
  • Unruly horses. Katha 1.3.3-6 describes the senses as horses, which run wild when the driver (the intellect) is untrained and the reins (the mind) slack.
  • Outward gaze. Katha 2.1.1 says the senses were turned outward, so people react to what happens outside and miss what is within.
  • Ignorance of unity. Isha 6-7: the one who sees all as the Self has no hatred, so the one who does not see this is open to it.
