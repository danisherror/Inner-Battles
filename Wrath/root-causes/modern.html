Root causes

Anger usually comes from how we read a situation, plus our state at the time.

  • Perceived wrong or threat. Anger tends to follow the thought "that was unfair" or "they disrespected me."
  • Blocked goals and stress. Tiredness, hunger, pain, noise, heat and overload lower the threshold.
  • Hurt underneath. Anger often covers fear, shame, grief or feeling powerless, which are harder to admit.
  • Rumination. Replaying a grievance keeps the feeling alive long after the event.
  • Learned patterns. Many people copy the way anger was handled around them as children.
  • Sense of entitlement and rigid "shoulds." Rules such as "people must treat me right" produce frequent anger.
  • Substances such as alcohol reduce self-control.
