Burnout and apathy

Exhaustion that flattens motivation.

Root causes

The Buddhist texts name tension and slackness in energy, and the way attention feeds them. AN 6.55: "when energy is too forceful it leads to restlessness. When energy is too slack it leads to laziness."

SN 46.51 says what fuels dullness: "There is discontent, sloth, yawning, sleepiness after eating, and mental sluggishness. Frequent irrational application of mind to that fuels the arising of dullness and drowsiness".

This is related to burnout and not identical to it. The texts are about a monk's practice, and "irrational application of mind" is the translator's phrase for a way of attending. Reading them as roots of burnout is ours, and they should not be read as saying that burnout is a failure of attention. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and exhaustion that is heavy or lasting is worth telling someone about.
