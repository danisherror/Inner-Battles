How to overcome it

The philosophers' counsel is to rest in order to work, and to alternate.

  1. Rest so that you can act. Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics 10.6: "to amuse oneself in order that one may exert oneself, as Anacharsis puts it, seems right; for amusement is a sort of relaxation, and we need relaxation because we cannot work continuously."
  2. Do not force the field. Seneca, Of Peace of Mind: "We must not force crops from rich fields, for an unbroken course of heavy crops will soon exhaust their fertility".
  3. Alternate. Same passage: "pass our lives alternately in solitude and among throngs of people".

This is related to burnout and not identical to it. Both are advice for a healthy mind, and neither describes recovery from exhaustion. Reading them as a way through burnout is ours, and nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and exhaustion that is heavy or lasting is worth telling someone about.
