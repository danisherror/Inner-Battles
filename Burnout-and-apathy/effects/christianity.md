The Bible names the weariness: "Come to me, all you who are weary and are carrying heavy burdens" (Matthew 11:28). Isaiah 40:30: "Even youths will faint and be weary, and the young will fall exhausted".

Exodus 18:18: "You will surely wear yourself out, both you and these people with you".

This is related to burnout and not identical to it. The effects shown are fainting, weariness and wearing out, and they reach the people around the one who is spent. Reading them as effects of burnout is ours. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and exhaustion that is heavy or lasting is worth telling someone about.
