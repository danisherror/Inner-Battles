Hinduism lens

What it is

Hinduism has no single word in the Bhagavad Gita for burnout, but it names both ends of a strained life. Gita 6.16: "those who eat too much or too little, sleep too much or too little, cannot attain success in Yog."

On the other side, 2.47: "You have a right to perform your prescribed duties, but you are not entitled to the fruits of your actions. Never consider yourself to be the cause of the results of your activities, nor be attached to inaction."

This is related to burnout and not identical to it. 6.16 is advice on the discipline of yoga and 2.47 is about working without clinging to results, and neither describes exhaustion as a state. Reading them as an account of burnout is ours. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and exhaustion that is heavy or lasting is worth telling someone about.
