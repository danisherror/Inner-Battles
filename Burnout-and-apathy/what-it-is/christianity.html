What it is

The Bible names being worn out. Jethro says to Moses: "What you are doing is not good. You will surely wear yourself out, both you and these people with you, for the task is too heavy for you; you cannot do it alone" (Exodus 18:17-18).

Isaiah 40:30: "Even youths will faint and be weary, and the young will fall exhausted".

And Psalm 127:2: "It is in vain that you rise up early and go late to rest, eating the bread of anxious toil, for he gives sleep to his beloved."

This is related to burnout and not identical to it. These passages describe the state and its cause without a name for it. The tradition's own word for a weariness of spirit, acedia, is not read here and is discussed in Sloth. Reading the passages as an account of burnout is ours. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and exhaustion that is heavy or lasting is worth telling someone about.
