Burnout and apathy

Exhaustion that flattens motivation.

What it is

The Buddhist texts name dullness as one of the five hindrances. SN 46.51 says what fuels "dullness and drowsiness": "There is discontent, sloth, yawning, sleepiness after eating, and mental sluggishness."

AN 6.55 names the two ways energy goes wrong: "when energy is too forceful it leads to restlessness. When energy is too slack it leads to laziness."

This is related to burnout and not identical to it. The texts are about practice and not about work, and "sloth" and "laziness" are the translator's words for states that are not the same as exhaustion from strain. Reading them as an account of burnout is ours. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and exhaustion that is heavy or lasting is worth telling someone about.
