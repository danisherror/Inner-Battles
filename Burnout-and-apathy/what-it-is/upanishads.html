What it is

The Upanishads show depletion in their ladder of "better than". Chandogya 7.9: "Food (anna) is better than power. Therefore if a man abstain from food for ten days, though he live, he would be unable to see, hear, perceive, think, act, and understand."

And 7.8: "Power (bala) is better than understanding. One powerful man shakes a hundred men of understanding. If a man is powerful, he becomes a rising man."

This is related to burnout and not identical to it. The passage is about food and power as steps in a teaching, and does not speak of work or exhaustion. Reading it as an account of burnout is ours. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and exhaustion that is heavy or lasting is worth telling someone about.
