What it is

Aristotle says we cannot work without stopping. Nicomachean Ethics 10.6: "amusement is a sort of relaxation, and we need relaxation because we cannot work continuously."

Seneca names what unbroken work does: "continuous toil produces a sort of numbness and sluggishness."

This is related to burnout and not identical to it. Aristotle is explaining why we amuse ourselves, and Seneca is writing about the mind's need for rest. Neither describes a syndrome. Reading them as an account of burnout is ours. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and exhaustion that is heavy or lasting is worth telling someone about.
