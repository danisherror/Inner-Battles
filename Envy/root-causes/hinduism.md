Hindu thought traces envy to **separateness, desire and the guna of rajas**.

- **Duality.** Seeing "me" and "them" as fundamentally apart makes another's gain feel like my loss. Gita 5.18 praises the sage who sees with an equal eye in all.
- **Unfulfilled desire.** Envy follows when desire is aimed at what others have. Gita 2.62-63 describes how fixing the mind on objects leads to desire, anger and delusion.
- **Ego.** Gita 16.18 links *asuya* with *ahamkara*, power and arrogance.
- **Lack of contentment.** Gita 12.19 describes the dear devotee as content with whatever comes. Envy is the opposite.
- **Karma as a lens.** Another's fortune is often explained by their past actions, which in tradition is a reason not to resent it.
