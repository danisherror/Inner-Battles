Root causes

Envy grows from comparison and from what comparison says about you.

  • Social comparison. People judge themselves by looking at others (Festinger's theory). Envy is strongest toward people close to us in age, field and background.
  • Self-relevance. We envy most in areas that matter to our self-image. Nobody envies a stranger's talent in something they don't care about.
  • Low or fragile self-worth. Someone else's success feels like proof of your own shortfall.
  • Feeling blocked. Envy is stronger when we believe the gap can't be closed, and turns malicious when it seems unfair.
  • Social media. Constant, curated views of other people's best moments feed comparison. Some studies link passive scrolling with lower mood.
