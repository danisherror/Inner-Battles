Root causes

For the Upanishads, the root is the mistaken sense that you are a separate, lacking self.

  • Fear and lack from a "second." Brihadaranyaka 1.4.2-3 says the Self felt fear because there was a second, and desire for that second. Where there is another, there is comparing and wanting.
  • The two birds. Mundaka 3.1.1-2: the bird caught up in the fruit grieves in its helplessness. The grief belongs to forgetting the other bird, the witness, which is the same Self.
  • Ignorance of unity. Isha 7: for the one who knows all beings have become the Self, there is no delusion or sorrow, so the lack of that knowledge is the cause.
  • Valuing outer things. Katha 2.1.1 says people look outward and not within, so they measure themselves by what they see in others.
