Envy damages the mind, and the community.

- **No concentration.** Dhammapada 365 says that a monk who envies others' gains does not attain concentration.
- **Unwholesome action.** Envy rooted in aversion leads to harsh speech and harmful acts, which in the teaching on karma bring suffering to oneself and others.
- **Quarrels.** Envy and stinginess feed disputes (the "quarrels and disputes" of the Kalahavivada Sutta, Sn 4.11, are traced to what is dear to us and to stinginess).
- **Splits in the Sangha.** The tradition's best-known case is Devadatta, whose envy of the Buddha's standing led him to attempt to divide the community (Vinaya, Cullavagga 7).
- **Lost joy.** Where *mudita* is absent, others' happiness is a source of pain, not delight.
