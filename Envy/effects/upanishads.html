Effects on life

In the Upanishads the effect of envy is the same as that of any sense of separateness: sorrow.

  • The grieving bird. Mundaka 3.1.2 says the one who is attached "grieves, deluded in his helplessness" until he sees the Lord and his greatness.
  • Fear and delusion. Isha 6-7 says that only the one who sees unity is free from shrinking, delusion and sorrow. The rest keep them.
  • Wandering. Katha 2.1.10 (also Brihadaranyaka 4.4.19) says one goes from death to death who sees difference here.
  • No peace. Katha 2.2.13 says eternal peace belongs to those who perceive the one Self, not to others.
