Scripture is stark about what envy does.

- **Wasting the body.** Proverbs 14:30: "jealousy makes the bones rot" (older NRSV: "passion").
- **Disorder.** James 3:14-16: where there is envy and selfish ambition, there is disorder and "wickedness of every kind."
- **Violence.** Cain's envy of Abel's accepted offering ends in murder (Genesis 4:3-8). Pilate "realized that it was out of jealousy that they had handed him over" (Matthew 27:18).
- **Exclusion from the community's peace.** Galatians 5:19-21 lists envy among the works of the flesh, and Galatians 5:26 urges, "Let us not become conceited, competing against one another, envying one another."
- **Contrast.** Romans 12:15 asks believers to "rejoice with those who rejoice."
