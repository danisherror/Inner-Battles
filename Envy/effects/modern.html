Effects on life

Envy hurts the envier first.

  • Mood. Envy goes with lower well-being, resentment and rumination.
  • Relationships. Resentment of friends and colleagues sours closeness; people feel the coldness even if you say nothing.
  • Behaviour. Malicious envy can lead to gossip, undermining or withholding help. In experiments, people have sometimes paid a cost to bring down a better-off partner.
  • Wasted signal. Benign envy can point to something you value, but malicious envy turns that into spite and wastes the information.
  • Brain. One imaging study found that envy activates a region linked to pain, and that the rival's misfortune activates reward areas.
