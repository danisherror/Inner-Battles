Envy

How to overcome it

Christian practice answers envy with gratitude, love and rejoicing with others.

  • Rejoice with those who rejoice (Romans 12:15). Start by praying for the person you envy.
  • Love. 1 Corinthians 13:4 says love "is not envious or boastful or arrogant." Practise the actions of love and the feeling often follows.
  • Gratitude. 1 Thessalonians 5:18 and 1 Corinthians 4:7 point to what has been received.
  • Trust God's generosity. Matthew 20:15 asks "Are you envious because I am generous?", and the answer is to accept grace given to others.
  • Contentment. Philippians 4:11-13: "I have learned to be content with whatever I have."
  • Different gifts. 1 Corinthians 12:14-26 says the body has many parts, and "If one member suffers, all suffer together with it; if one member is honored, all rejoice together with it."
  • Confession. Naming the sin in confession or to a trusted person takes away its secrecy.
