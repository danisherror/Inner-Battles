The Upanishadic cure is to see the one Self in all.

- **See all in the Self.** Isha 6-7: the one who sees all beings in the Self and the Self in all beings no longer shrinks, and for them delusion and sorrow end.
- **Do not covet.** Isha 1 gives the practice: leave the wealth of others to them and enjoy through renunciation.
- **Take the witness seat.** The second bird of Mundaka 3.1.1-2 looks on without eating. Practise watching your own wants.
- **Study and reflect.** The three steps of *shravana*, *manana* and *nididhyasana* (later Vedanta, built on Brihadaranyaka 2.4.5) turn knowledge of unity into experience.
- **Give and be kind.** Taittiriya 1.11 advises giving in faith and with sympathy.
