How to overcome it

The aim is to turn envy into information and then into goodwill.

  1. Name it. Say "this is envy." Unnamed envy shows up as irritation or criticism.
  2. Ask what it points to. What do I actually want? A skill, recognition, security? Envy is often a signal of a value.
  3. Convert it to a step. If the want is reachable, choose one action (a course, a conversation, a draft). This is the benign form.
  4. Cut the fuel. Mute or unfollow accounts that leave you feeling behind, and cut passive scrolling.
  5. Remember the unseen. You see their result, not their cost, doubts or luck.
  6. Practise active joy. Tell the person congratulations and mean it. Do it even when it's hard.
  7. Keep a gratitude list, and compare yourself with your past self.
