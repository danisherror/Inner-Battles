Envy

How to overcome it

Buddhism trains the opposite of envy: mudita, sympathetic joy.

  • Practise mudita. Bring to mind someone who is doing well and wish: "May your good fortune continue." Start with someone easy and move toward the person you envy. This is one of the four brahmaviharas (MN 7).
  • Metta. Loving-kindness for all beings (Sn 1.8, Karaniya Metta Sutta) softens the aversion that envy grows from.
  • Be content. Dhammapada 365 says do not despise what you have received or envy others' gains.
  • Notice the eight winds. AN 8.6: gain and loss, praise and blame are not the measure of a life. Observe envy as it comes and goes.
  • Dedicate merit. Rejoicing in others' good deeds (anumodana) is a standard practice in Buddhist communities.
  • See not-self. SN 22.59 loosens the idea of a fixed "me" to be ranked.
