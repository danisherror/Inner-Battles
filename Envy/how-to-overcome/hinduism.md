Hindu practice answers envy with equal vision, friendliness and contentment.

- **Equal vision.** Gita 5.18 and 6.9 praise the one who sees all with an equal eye, and 6.32 the one who feels another's joy and sorrow as their own.
- **Friendliness and compassion.** Gita 12.13: "adveshta sarva-bhutanam, maitrah karuna eva cha", hating no being, friendly and compassionate. The Yoga Sutras (1.33) recommend friendliness toward the happy, compassion toward the unhappy, gladness toward the virtuous and equanimity toward the wicked.
- **Contentment.** Gita 12.19 and Yoga Sutras 2.42 (*santosha*).
- **Offer your actions.** Gita 3.30 and 18.66 teach acting for the Divine and letting go of "mine."
- **Satsang and service**, which put attention on others' good.
