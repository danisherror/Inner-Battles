The Sanskrit word is **matsarya**, envy or jealousy of others' good fortune, the sixth of the inner enemies (*shadripu*). Related words are *asuya* (finding fault, begrudging) and *irshya* (jealousy).

The Gita names the quality in its portrait of the *asuri* nature: in 16.18, such people hate the Divine in themselves and in others, and are full of *asuya*. By contrast, the person dear to Krishna is described as "hating no creature" (12.13), and in 3.31 those who follow the teaching with faith and "without envy" (*anasuyantah*) are freed from the bonds of action. The wise student is called *anasuya* ("free from envy") in 9.1 and 18.71.
