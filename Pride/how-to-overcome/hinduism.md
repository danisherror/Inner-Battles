Hindu practice answers pride with humility, service and surrender of the fruits of action.

- **Cultivate *amanitvam*.** Gita 13.8 starts the list of knowledge with humility and absence of pretence, and goes on to respect for the teacher.
- **Act without claiming the result.** Gita 2.47 says you have a right to action but not to its fruits. Gita 3.30 asks you to offer actions to the Divine, free from "mine" and from fever of ego.
- **Learn by serving the teacher.** Gita 4.34 advises seeking knowledge by humble inquiry and service.
- **Bhakti.** Bhakti traditions teach seeing yourself as a servant. In Gita 12.13-14, the devotee dear to Krishna is free of "mine" and "I", and is forgiving and content.
- **Practise seva and charity**, which put others' needs first.
