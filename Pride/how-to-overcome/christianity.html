How to overcome it

Christian teaching on humility is concrete.

  • Follow Christ's example. Philippians 2:5-8 describes him as emptying himself and taking the form of a servant. John 13:1-17 shows him washing his disciples' feet.
  • Pray honestly. The tax collector's "God, be merciful to me, a sinner" (Luke 18:13) is the model. Confession and repentance remove the pretence.
  • Count others higher. Philippians 2:3 and Romans 12:10 ask you to outdo one another in showing honour.
  • Receive correction. Proverbs 12:1 says whoever loves discipline loves knowledge.
  • Give thanks. 1 Corinthians 4:7 asks, "What do you have that you did not receive?"
  • Serve in hidden ways. Matthew 6:1-4 says to give without announcing it.

The tradition's name for the virtue is humilitas, from humus (earth), meaning truthful grounded-ness, not self-contempt.
