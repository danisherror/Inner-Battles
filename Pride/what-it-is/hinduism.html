Pride

Valuing yourself above truth, and above other people.

What it is

Hinduism has several words for pride, each with a different shade. Ahamkara is the "I-maker", the sense of being a separate self that owns and does things. Mada is intoxication with one's own status or power, and it is one of the six inner enemies (shadripu). Darpa and abhimana mean arrogance and conceit.

The Gita lists them plainly. In 16.4, Krishna names hypocrisy, arrogance (darpa), conceit (abhimana), anger, harshness and ignorance as the marks of those drawn toward the asuri (demonic) nature. In 16.13-15 he gives the voice of such a person: "This much I have gained today... I am rich and well-born. Who is equal to me?"

At the root is the error in 3.27: all action is done by the gunas (the qualities of nature), yet the person deluded by ahamkara thinks, "I am the doer."

Humility is the opposite. In 13.8, amanitvam (absence of self-regard) heads the list of marks of true knowledge.
