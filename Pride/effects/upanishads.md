The Upanishads describe the cost of pride as staying lost in ignorance.

- **Going round in circles.** Katha 1.2.5 and Mundaka 1.2.8 say those who think themselves wise while ignorant "wander around, deluded, like blind men led by the blind." Learning has become a trap.
- **Sorrow despite knowledge.** Chandogya 7.1 has Narada admit that although he knows much, he is in sorrow because he does not know the Self. Pride in learning does not remove grief.
- **Separation.** Isha 6-7 says the one who sees all beings in the Self, and the Self in all beings, feels no hatred or delusion. By contrast, the proud person who insists on difference keeps both.
- **Missing the teaching.** Kena 2.3 holds that the one who is sure he knows, does not know. Certainty ends the search before it has begun.
