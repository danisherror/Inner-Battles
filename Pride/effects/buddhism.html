Deadly sin

Pride

Valuing yourself above truth, and above other people.

Effects on life

Conceit keeps a person bound to suffering, and spoils how they treat others.

  • It blocks release. In the traditional list of ten fetters, conceit (mana) is one of the last to fall, so a practitioner can be far along and still held by it. SN 22.89 describes this.
  • Heedlessness. AN 3.39 says intoxication with youth, health and life leads people to wrong conduct and, afterwards, regret.
  • Unwelcome results. MN 135 says arrogance and disrespect toward the worthy lead to lower rebirth, a teaching about the results of action, not about any group of people.
  • Loneliness. The Dhammapada (verse 74) shows the person who wants every deed credited to them: the wish to be seen as the doer breeds craving and conceit.

Buddhism treats the effect as a loop: the more you defend the "I", the more it suffers when it is challenged.
