Hubristic pride costs more than it seems to give.

- **Relationships.** Arrogant people are liked less over time. They interrupt, dismiss and rarely apologise, so friends and partners stop being honest with them.
- **Work.** If you can't admit errors or take feedback, you repeat them. Teams stop sharing bad news with a leader who punishes it.
- **Learning.** Believing you already know blocks growth. Research on intellectual humility links it with better learning and more open-minded thinking.
- **Decisions.** Overconfidence leads to risks taken without checking, as in the leaders described in "hubris syndrome."
- **Inner life.** Pride feels strong but is brittle: criticism hurts more, and the need to keep up appearances is tiring.
