Pride

Valuing yourself above truth, and above other people.

Effects on life

The Gita describes where pride leads.

  • Rejecting good advice. Gita 18.58 says that if, out of ahamkara, you do not listen, you will perish. Pride closes the ear to the teacher.
  • Bondage to the doer idea. In 3.27 the person who thinks "I am the doer" stays tied to the results of action, so success inflates and failure crushes.
  • Descent. Gita 16.16-20 follows the arrogant and wealth-driven mind to anxiety, anger and, finally, rebirth in lower states. The chapter calls them "the lowest of men."
  • Loss of peace. Gita 2.71 says peace comes to one who moves free from longing, without the sense of "mine" (nirmama) and "I" (nirahankara). Pride is the reverse.

The epics tell the same story in action: Ravana and Duryodhana both lose everything after refusing the counsel of those who loved them.
