Pride

Valuing yourself above truth, and above other people.

Effects on life

Scripture and tradition describe pride's cost as a fall, and as broken relationships.

  • A fall. Proverbs 16:18 ("Pride goes before destruction") and Proverbs 11:2 ("When pride comes, then comes disgrace; but wisdom is with the humble") state the pattern simply.
  • Opposition. James 4:6 says God "opposes the proud." The proud person refuses grace because it feels like an admission of need.
  • Contempt for others. Luke 18:9 says Jesus told the parable of the Pharisee and tax collector "to some who trusted in themselves that they were righteous and regarded others with contempt."
  • Blindness to your own faults. Matthew 7:3-5 asks why you notice the speck in a neighbour's eye but not the log in your own.
  • Dividing the community. Philippians 2:3 urges "in humility regard others as better than yourselves," and Paul sets this against "selfish ambition or conceit."
