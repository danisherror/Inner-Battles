Deadly sin

Pride

Valuing yourself above truth, and above other people.

Root causes

For the Upanishads, pride grows from avidya, ignorance of what you really are.

  • Confusing the self with the body-mind. Brihadaranyaka 1.4.1 describes the first sense of "I am" arising, and 1.4.2 says the Self then feared, because there was a second. The separate "I" is where both pride and fear start.
  • Knowing things instead of knowing the Self. In Chandogya 7.1, Narada tells Sanatkumara he has learned all the Vedas and sciences yet is still a "knower of words only," not of the Self, and so is in sorrow.
  • Intellect cannot settle it. Katha 1.2.23 says the Self is not gained by teaching, by intellect or by much learning. The learned who think this is enough are the ones Katha 1.2.5 calls blind.
  • Taking the knower for the known. Kena 1.3-4 says the Self cannot be grasped by the eye, speech or mind, so the mind that claims to have mastered it has missed it.
