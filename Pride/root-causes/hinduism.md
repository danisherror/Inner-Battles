Hindu teaching traces pride to **ahamkara**, the sense of "I" and "mine", and to the way it grows when the mind is ruled by *rajas* and *tamas* instead of *sattva*.

- **Misidentifying the doer.** Gita 3.27 says the deluded person, with the mind confused by ego, thinks "I am the doer," when actions are carried out by the *gunas*.
- **Wealth, power and craving.** Gita 16.13-16 shows how desire and the thought "I have gained this, I will gain that" lead to arrogance and to being "bewildered by many thoughts."
- **Inner enemies feed each other.** Pride (*mada*) sits among the six *shadripu* beside desire, anger, greed and delusion. Success in one tends to feed the others.
- **Forgetting the source.** Gita 10.8 and 18.61 describe the Lord as the source of everything and as dwelling in the heart, so claiming achievements as purely your own is a kind of forgetting.
