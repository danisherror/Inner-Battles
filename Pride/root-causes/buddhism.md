Buddhism explains conceit through **craving, ignorance and the idea of a fixed self**.

- **Belief in a self.** Because we cling to a sense of "I", we compare it with others. The Anattalakkhana Sutta (SN 22.59) teaches that the five aggregates are not self, which removes the thing conceit is built on.
- **Intoxication with advantages.** AN 3.39 names the pride of youth, of health and of life, and says it makes people act badly in body, speech and mind.
- **Karmic habit.** MN 135 (Culakammavibhanga Sutta) says that being arrogant and haughty, and not honouring those worthy of honour, leads to a low birth. The sutta frames this as the result of actions, not a verdict on any group of people today.
- **Residue even in the advanced.** SN 22.89 shows the "I am" conceit survives until the final stage, so pride is not only for the crude.
