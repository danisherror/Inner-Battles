**Nebuchadnezzar's fall**

Daniel chapter 4 tells of King Nebuchadnezzar of Babylon. He had a dream, and Daniel warned him that it meant he would be humbled unless he changed: "atone for your sins with righteousness and your iniquities with mercy to the oppressed."

A year later, walking on the roof of his palace, the king said, "Is this not magnificent Babylon, which I have built as a royal capital by my mighty power and for my glorious majesty?"

While the words were still in his mouth, a voice said his kingdom had been taken from him. He was driven away from people and lived like an animal for a time ("seven times").

At the end he looked up and his reason returned. He blessed the Most High and said that "all his works are truth, and his ways are justice; he is able to bring low those who walk in pride." His kingdom was restored.

**What it shows:** The line "I have built" is the sin in a sentence. His recovery began when he looked up and gave credit.
