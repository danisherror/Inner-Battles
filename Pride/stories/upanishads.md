**The Yaksha and the blade of grass**

The Kena Upanishad (Parts 3-4) tells of a battle in which Brahman won the victory for the gods. The gods, though, believed the victory was their own: "Ours is this victory, ours this greatness."

Brahman saw this and appeared before them as a mysterious *yaksha* (a spirit). They did not know what it was. They sent Agni, the fire god, to find out. The yaksha asked, "What power do you have?" Agni said, "I can burn anything on earth." The yaksha put down a blade of grass and said, "Burn this." Agni used all his strength and could not.

Vayu, the wind god, came next and could not blow it away. Last came Indra, and the yaksha vanished. In its place stood the goddess Uma, who told Indra the spirit was Brahman, and that the victory belonged to Brahman.

**What it shows:** Powers that feel like your own are borrowed. Pride is the mistake of taking a gift for a possession, and the lesson ends in recognition, not punishment.
