Hinduism has no single word in the Bhagavad Gita for fear of failure or of success, but the Gita speaks of treating them alike. Gita 2.38: "Fight for the sake of duty, treating alike happiness and distress, loss and gain, victory and defeat."

Gita 4.22 describes the person "content with whatever gain comes of its own accord, and free from envy", who is "equipoised in success and failure" and so "not bound by their actions, even while performing all kinds of activities".

This is related to fear of failure and success and not identical to it. The Gita is describing equanimity toward results, and it does not describe the dread of the attempt. Reading it as speaking to that dread is ours.
