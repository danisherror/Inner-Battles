Fear of failure and success

Staying out of the attempt because you dread falling short, or dread what succeeding would bring.

Upanishads lens

What it is

The Upanishads do not name fear of failure or of success, and the closest they come is the idea of acting without clinging to the result. Isha Upanishad 2: "Though a man may wish to live a hundred years, performing works, it will be thus with him; but not in any other way: work will thus not cling to a man."

The verse follows the instruction to give up the sense of ownership (Isha 1) and then to act: work does not cling to the one who acts in this way.

This is related to fear of failure and success and not identical to it. The verse is about action and non-attachment, and does not describe fear of the attempt. Reading it as speaking to that fear is ours.
