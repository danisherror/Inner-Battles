The Bible does not name fear of failure, but it speaks of falling and rising. Proverbs 24:16: "for though they fall seven times, they will rise again, but the wicked are overthrown by calamity."

And Paul writes of weakness: "My grace is sufficient for you, for power is made perfect in weakness" (2 Corinthians 12:9).

This is related to fear of failure and success and not identical to it. The verses speak of falling and of weakness, and not of the dread that stops a person trying. Reading them as speaking to that dread is ours.
