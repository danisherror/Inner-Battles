Fear of failure and success

Staying out of the attempt because you dread falling short, or dread what succeeding would bring.

Effects on life

The Gita shows what follows when a person stops. Arjuna, facing the battle, says "Govind, I shall not fight," and "became silent" (2.9).

Gita 18.8 names a related pattern: "To give up prescribed duties because they are troublesome or cause bodily discomfort is renunciation in the mode of passion. Such renunciation is never beneficial or elevating."

This is related to fear of failure and success and not identical to it. Arjuna's refusal comes from grief at the sight of his kinsmen, and 18.8 names trouble and discomfort and does not mention fear. Reading them as the effect of fear of failure is ours.
