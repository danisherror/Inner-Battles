The Bible's answer to failure is to rise, to be held, and to press on.

1. **Get up.** Proverbs 24:16: "for though they fall seven times, they will rise again".
2. **Hold on to being held.** Psalm 37:24: "though we stumble, we shall not fall headlong, for the Lord holds us by the hand."
3. **Speak to your accuser.** Micah 7:8: "Do not rejoice over me, my enemies; when I fall, I shall rise; when I sit in darkness, the Lord will be a light to me."
4. **Do not stay with what is behind.** Philippians 3:13-14: "forgetting what lies behind and straining forward to what lies ahead, I press on toward the goal".
5. **Let weakness be.** 2 Corinthians 12:9: "power is made perfect in weakness."

This is related to fear of failure and success and not identical to it. These verses are about falling and rising in general, and nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist for constant anxiety or low mood.
