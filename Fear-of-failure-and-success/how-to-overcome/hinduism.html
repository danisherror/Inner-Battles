Inner battle

Fear of failure and success

Staying out of the attempt because you dread falling short, or dread what succeeding would bring.

How to overcome it

The Gita points to doing the work and loosening the grip on the result.

  1. Do the duty, not the outcome. Gita 2.47: "You have a right to perform your prescribed duties, but you are not entitled to the fruits of your actions."
  2. Do not slide into inaction. The same verse adds: "nor be attached to inaction."
  3. Stay level. Gita 2.48: "Be steadfast in the performance of your duty, O Arjun, abandoning attachment to success and failure. Such equanimity is called Yog."
  4. Do not be thrown by loss or gain. Gita 12.17 describes those "who neither lament for any loss nor hanker for any gain".

This is related to fear of failure and success and not identical to it. The verses are about equanimity in action in general, and nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist for constant anxiety or low mood. Reading them as advice for the dread of an attempt is ours.
