The Upanishads point to acting with a loosened grip, and to choosing what matters.

1. **Let go of ownership, then act.** Isha 1: "When thou hast surrendered all this, then thou mayest enjoy." Isha 2: "work will thus not cling to a man."
2. **Choose the good over the pleasant.** Katha 1.2.2: "the wise prefers the good to the pleasant".
3. **Do not be pulled away.** Of Nachiketas, Death says: "even many pleasures did not tear thee away" (Katha 1.2.4).

This is related to fear of failure and success and not identical to it. These verses are about non-attachment and choosing the good, and nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist for constant anxiety or low mood. Reading them as advice for the dread of an attempt is ours.
