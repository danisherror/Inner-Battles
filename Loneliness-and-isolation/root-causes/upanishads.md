The Upanishads place the root of sorrow in seeing something other than the Self. Chandogya 7.24: "Where one sees something else, hears something else, understands something else, that is the finite. The Infinite is immortal, the finite is mortal."

Isha 7 says what the other seeing removes: "what sorrow, what trouble can there be to him who once beheld that unity?"

This is related to loneliness and not identical to it. The root named is a way of seeing and not a lack of company, and reading it as a root of loneliness is ours. It should not be read as saying that a lonely person only needs to see differently. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and loneliness that is heavy or lasting is worth telling someone about.
