Hinduism lens

Root causes

The Gita names what the beloved devotee is free from. 12.13-14: "Those devotees are very dear to Me who are free from malice toward all living beings, who are friendly, and compassionate. They are free from attachment to possessions and egotism, equipoised in happiness and distress, and ever-forgiving."

By implication, malice, attachment and egotism are what stand between a person and others.

This is related to loneliness and not identical to it. The verses describe a person dear to God and do not discuss loneliness, and the inference that malice and egotism cause it is ours. It should not be read as saying that lonely people are malicious or proud. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and loneliness that is heavy or lasting is worth telling someone about.
