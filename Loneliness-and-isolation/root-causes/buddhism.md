The Buddhist texts count separation as suffering. SN 56.11: "being coupled with the disliked is suffering; separation from the liked is suffering; not getting what you wish for is suffering."

SN 45.2 shows what it means to have the opposite: "A mendicant with good friends, companions, and associates can expect to develop and cultivate the noble eightfold path."

This is related to loneliness and not identical to it. SN 56.11 lists kinds of suffering and does not name loneliness, though separation from those we like comes close to it, and SN 45.2 is about the practice of the path. Reading them as a root of loneliness is ours. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and loneliness that is heavy or lasting is worth telling someone about.
