The Upanishads name the lifting of sorrow as the effect of seeing unity. Isha 7: "what sorrow, what trouble can there be to him who once beheld that unity?" Chandogya 7.25: "He who sees, perceives, and understands this, loves the Self, delights in the Self, revels in the Self, rejoices in the Self".

This is related to loneliness and not identical to it. The passages are about the knower and not about the lonely, and reading them as the answer to loneliness is ours. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and loneliness that is heavy or lasting is worth telling someone about.
