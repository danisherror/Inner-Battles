How to overcome it

The Buddhist texts counsel seeking an alert companion, and failing that a blameless solitude.

  1. Look for a good friend. SN 45.2: "Good friends, companions, and associates are the whole of the spiritual life."
  2. Walk with an alert one. Dhp 328: "If you find an alert companion, an attentive friend to live happily together, then, overcoming all adversities, wander with them, joyful and mindful."
  3. If you cannot, be at ease alone. Dhp 330: "Wander alone and do no wrong, at ease like a tusker in the wilds."

This is related to loneliness and not identical to it. The verses also say "there's no fellowship with fools", which is the text's own wording and should not be read as calling lonely people fools. Dhp 330 recommends solitude and is not advice on how to ease an ache. Reading the verses as a way through loneliness is ours, and nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and loneliness that is heavy or lasting is worth telling someone about.
