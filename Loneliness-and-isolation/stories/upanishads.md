**Seeing all beings in the Self**

In Isha 6-7 a teacher says: "he who beholds all beings in the Self, and the Self in all beings, he never turns away from it. When to a man who understands, the Self has become all things, what sorrow, what trouble can there be to him who once beheld that unity?"

**What it shows:** The verse holds up a person for whom nothing is other, and so nothing left out. It is a picture and not a tale, and linking it to loneliness is ours. It should not be read as saying that a lonely person is missing something. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and loneliness that is heavy or lasting is worth telling someone about.
