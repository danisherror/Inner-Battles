**A seat in seclusion, and a friend of all**

Two verses of the Gita put solitude and company side by side. In 6.10: "Those who seek the state of Yog should reside in seclusion, constantly engaged in meditation with a controlled mind and body, getting rid of desires and possessions for enjoyment."

In 5.29 the Lord is described as "the selfless friend of all living beings": "My devotee attains peace."

**What it shows:** The yogi alone in seclusion is not described as abandoned, since the same text names a friend of all beings. This is a picture drawn from two verses and not a tale of a lonely person, and linking it to loneliness is ours. Nothing here replaces a doctor or therapist, and loneliness that is heavy or lasting is worth telling someone about.
